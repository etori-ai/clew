# Implementation status: work, decisions and durable execution

One page of [implementation status](../status.md). Each entry says what works in this checkout, what limits it or is still to do, and where the code and tests that show it are.

## Approved writes

**Works in this checkout.** Generic card decisions and calendar create/move/cancel/confirm/undo use encrypted intent, transactional audit/card projection and workflow start (D86), read-back and linked compensation. Calendar API writes require a stable `decisionId` and return a pending card. A card decision is on the version of the item the person was shown: `POST /api/threads/:id/act` and a planning card's `POST /api/threads/:id/confirm` carry the item's `updatedAt` (`version`), and an action's dialog sends the version the item had when it opened. An execute or a confirmation on an item that has changed since is refused with "Ärendet har ändrats sedan du öppnade det. Se över det och besluta igen." and the page reads the items again; after that refusal the dialog's next send is on the item as it now draws it. A decision already made on the item is replayed whatever version it names. The entity menu's execute names no version: its card is the one of the dialog's action, input and answers, so what is sent is what the dialog showed. A decision that ended refused (`failed`) or in part (`partial`) leaves the item to be decided again, as a new decision on the item as it is now: the notice's move is the item's own decision (or the review's page for a reviewed one), and the rows the source got done, by any earlier decision on the item as the ledger records them, are not chosen and are refused if sent ("En del av raderna är redan genomförda. Välj bara de som återstår."). A decision whose outcome stays `unknown` is ended only by its own person: under ⋯ "Markera som inte genomfört" asks first, saying it is only for a decision the person has seen in the source did not go through, then settles it with the reason `released-by-person` (`POST /api/threads/:id/execution/release`, audited `execution.released` in their name, nothing sent), `failed`, or `partial` when the source had confirmed rows, which stay done and can still be undone; the item says "Markerat som inte genomfört" and is decided again. A decision held back in doubt (a sign-in, an authority or the source's terms) can be let go the same way, and the endpoint also lets go one left `running` past its lease. One being read back right now is refused ("Beslutet kontrolleras just nu. Försök igen om en stund."), an undo in doubt is never let go, and Clew never ends a doubt itself (D45). The planning card offers the same, and after a refused, partial or released decision it sends the times that remain and draws another week again; after an undo the calendar refused it does not offer Ångra again, since the same undo would be that refusal replayed. Undoing sent invitations makes their times proposed again, so the planning card offers "Skicka" and "Vecka N i stället" once more. "Kontrollera status" also starts again a decision whose workflow ended in `ERROR`, one still waiting to be sent or one left running past its lease, and each check is a reconciliation of its own, so a check after one that itself ended in `ERROR` still runs. When the source's answer cannot be recorded, that is logged by the error's name (scope `executions`) and tried again, never stored as the source not answering; after five attempts the decision stays `running` and is tried again only when it is checked, once its lease has run out. A reminder or snooze whose timer is stopped (blocked) no longer leaves its card parked: the card is back where it was, and a stopped reminder is said on its row ("påminnelsen stoppad"). A follow-up whose replies or team cannot be read checks again at its next time and says what it could not read in its audit row; while it cannot read the replies it reminds nobody, since an invitee may have answered. An effect is compensated at most once: an undo is the decision `undo:<original>` and no other decision may be, so a second undo of the same effect, from the item's ⋯ or from its receipt, is the first one replayed; the human decision endpoint `POST /api/executions` takes no compensation ("Ett beslut ångras från sitt ärende."), since an undo there would leave the item its receipt. A card decision takes the person's answers to the capability's `asks` (D112): checked against the asks, merged into the approved input and named in the approval's audit row ([what a write asks](protocol.md#what-a-write-asks-and-what-it-says-it-will-do)). An execute started about a person is approved with the user name the decision resolved for the person, and its approved digest carries the person (`subject`, migration 0056); dispatch sends it only while that user name is still the person's ([D122](../../decisions/D122-clew-keys-its-own-records-about-a-person-by-the-persons-id.md)). Otherwise, before anything was sent, it settles `failed` with the reason `subject-changed`: the work item says "Personen har inte längre det användarnamn som beslutet gällde. Inget skickades. Fatta beslutet igen." and its own button decides again under a new decision id, on the user name the person has now. After an attempt whose answer is missing it settles `unknown` with the same reason and is not sent again: "Personen har inte längre det användarnamn som beslutet gällde, så åtgärden skickas inte igen. Utfallet behöver kontrolleras i källsystemet." On a connector whose audience names a provisioned group, a member's approval is recorded and sent under the member's current groups, and one whose person has left the group by the time it runs is not sent (`authority-revoked`). An approved talk invitation takes a lock per invitee and is recorded on its proposal (migration 0056), so a second manager's confirmation of the same person is refused while the first may still be sent: running, pending (even with a blocking reason), or unknown, inside its approval's 24 hours, even if its manager leaves the team and can later return ([talks](people.md#utvecklingssamtal-talks)). When the calendar's own check right before sending finds the time or the room not free, or cannot read them, the invitation is not sent: its row says "Inget skickades: …" and the execution settles `failed`, not `unknown`.

**Limits and pending work.** Real-system pilot choice, live CalDAV regression of the new path and the full process-death matrix remain open. Unknown effects are not blindly replayed. An execute started from a person's menu is not sent to the new holder or under the new name when the person's user name moved before dispatch: when nothing was sent, the approver decides again, on the user name the person has now; after an attempt without an answer it is not sent again, and its outcome is to be checked in the source system.

## Jobs across systems

**Works in this checkout.** A connector declares the facts it creates (`facts`, `creates`) and the proposals that follow a fact kind (`follows`, taking a `FactEnvelope`, returning a `FollowResponse` whose steps name the follower's own connector); an administrator binds followers to a kind in the database (default deny); Clew assembles one job proposal by fan-out, names the outcome of every binding that is there for the person (inside the binding's audience and inside its connector's audience, whatever the connector's state; any other binding, and so any connector whose audience leaves them out, is left out and never named, D23; a connector switched off or no longer registered is named as degraded and the proposal is partial), and approves it once: one execution row per execute step, a bounded watch per watch step with its timer intent (`watch_intent`), undo-all as one linked compensation per step, all in one transaction with audit and the workflow starts (D86). One `jobWatch` workflow per watch step starts with that commit; it waits for each committed check, calls the fenced `observe` activity (read as the person, `seen` ends the watch, a revoked authority leaves it `blocked`), and at the window's end settles `expired`, which leaves one card for the person through the stream's attention admission.

**Limits and pending work.** Thin routes only (`POST /api/jobs/assemble`, `POST /api/jobs`, `GET /api/jobs/:id`): no card UI, panel step list or administration page for bindings; the job and its watches exist as ledger rows, audit, the API's view and the expired watch's generic pushed card. Bindings are made with `bun scripts/job-bind.ts` (or `--fixtures` for the development fixture offer) until the admin page has them. The independent fixture connectors declare the offer (`hr:absence`; `hr.proposeAbsence`, `timesheet.followAbsence`, `payroll.followAbsence`): payroll reads HR's authenticated HTTP feed and exposes reports dated before today, so a report made today is watched until the window ends. A watch is approved once and never replaced (generation 1); there is no cancel of a running watch. Fact-kind drift is judged at assembly (degraded bindings named in the proposal), not yet on the administration page. The full composer/pushed entry paths and receipt-driven facts assembling the next proposal remain planned; `creates` does not automatically start another job. See [planned continuation](../../architecture/working-across-connectors.md#planned-continuation-after-a-result). [D77](../../decisions/D77-job-approvals-preserve-intent-bindings-and-receipt-authority.md) closes the reproduced approval/read gaps: retries retain the original watch schedule, approval rechecks current binding revisions and per-binding limits, and receipt paths require the original unit/authorization version plus current capability access. Clients echo step `binding` and source `bindingRevision` from assembly; old unapproved proposals need reassembly. Immutable server-side reviewed proposals and creator-schema validation of facts remain unimplemented.

## Scan occurrence lifecycle

**Works in this checkout.** Persisted source key/assignment/period/revision, local dismissal, source-isolated coalesced refresh, stale-result fencing and authoritative completion. Partial/unavailable scans cannot infer completion by absence. Source cards retain their original person/grant version; transfers and renewed grants cannot read old snapshots. Fresh scans create separate authorized projections.

**Limits and pending work.** Legacy source cards without provenance are hidden after migration 0034; source-only ACL changes need a revocation path into Clew before persisted scans are enabled ([D75](../../decisions/D75-source-snapshots-retain-the-authority-that-produced-them.md)). Legacy topic-only scans stay conservative. Calendar's demonstration planner keeps its own proposal lifecycle.

## Timers

**Works in this checkout.** Transactional absolute timer intents, generations, cancellation and current-authority checks; every intent starts its workflow inside the transaction that commits it (D86: `dbos.enqueue_workflow` on the ledger's own connection, no outbox loop). The durable engine (DBOS Transact in the server process, D85) owns new and queued legacy jobs. Every scheduled job (the nightly scan, the nightly sign-in clean-up, the end of an undo window, a receipt's final state and an attention release) is a job row whose `scheduledJob` workflow starts in the transaction that writes it, and the engine runs it at its time through `scheduledJobRunner`; nothing polls the job table, and the polling scheduler is gone. A run renews its claim on the job while it lasts, so a run longer than the two-minute lease is never run twice beside itself, a job that stopped without completing is counted for the operator (`stalledJobs`), and a run still going after ten minutes is said in one error line and counted (`longRunningJobs`). The nightly scan runs 500 people to a page, each page a job of its own, so no run grows with the installation. A server's start takes over the last and the next night of the scan and of the clean-up, the later pages of the last night's scan included, when a deployment left them waiting under the replaced application version, so a deployment does not stop them and a night whose time fell during it runs late (D85 item 2, as amended 2026-11-13). `scripts/workflows-move.ts` lists and moves every other workflow left under an older version, from where its records end, and starts one that ended in `ERROR` again from the step that failed. A job's watch step is a bounded conditional watch: committed absolute checks and an end, read as the person, settled in the ledger (see Jobs across systems).

**Limits and pending work.** Legacy running jobs require operator drain evidence. Every other workflow that a deployment leaves waiting under the replaced application version (a reminder, an approved execution, a watch, a follow-up, a scheduled job other than the last and the next nights) runs only once an operator moves it with the script after the older processes have stopped ([after every deployment](../../maintainers/reliability-operations.md#after-every-deployment)); nothing moves it at start, since a start cannot tell whether those processes have stopped. Nothing times out a job's run: one that never settles holds its job until its process stops, so an operator restarts the instance that runs it ([operations](../../maintainers/reliability-operations.md#the-ledger-queue)). The watch is the one conditional timer: no general "tell me when X" outside a job, no cancel of a running watch; a live delivery deadline is not established. The retired outbox tables and columns remain as evidence until a later schema change drops them.

## Review recovery and completion

**Works in this checkout.** Submitted reviews remain openable from the card and Klart after undo expires. Optional connector-declared text corrections use the human execution ledger and reload the reviewed version only after confirmed success; a source link is the fallback. A deterministic new card retries rejected rows only, preserving the original receipt and confirmed writes. Unknown outcomes require reconciliation. Shared completion feedback covers reviews, receipts and successful saves; undo errors remain visible and status checking retains its controls.

**Limits and pending work.** Corrections currently support up to 12 bounded text fields, not arbitrary forms. The finance fixture demonstrates missing-order rejection, correction and retry; its source URL is a placeholder. Current documents are shown beside the original receipt, not an archived copy of the submitted bytes.

## Becoming done

**Works in this checkout.** When a receipt arrives the row keeps its place for 2,5 s in its receipt state (the check, the headline, Ångra), then goes to Klart, and a toast with Visa and Ångra says so; a connector may mark an execute `review: 'each'` with a `preview` read, and the work item's next step `Granska N` (and each document under its "Dokument") then leads to the review's own page, `/items/<threadId>/review` with the step as `step` and the page the item was shown on as `from` (D73, amended 2026-11-12 under the product owner's rule that a task a person works through is a page): the trail back, the title and the progress (a Stepper below five rows, a ProgressBar from five) in the header, one row at a time with its document, "Hittills" beside it (under it at 1024 px and below), the decision's buttons and Skicka in the footer, the page stacked on a phone; it writes once at the end and leads back to the item where it was shown, with the receipt and Ångra. The decisions are the person's draft for the session (session storage per work item, `apps/web/src/review-session.ts`): a reload, Back and Forward, or a way out and back keep them, the item then says "Fortsätt granska" and shows them as its ticks, and the draft is cleared on Skicka, when the reviewed rows change, and when the session ends; the review's evidence is never kept, so after a reload Skicka reads the approved rows' documents again and sends only the versions the approvals were taken on. Back from the page puts the focus on the control that led there. The overlay's address (`?review=<threadId>&step=` on any page) and `/cards/:threadId/review` lead to the page; a decided item keeps its receipt, with Ångra while it can be undone. The finance fixture now provides original PDF invoices, DOCX agreements and XLSX specifications through the versioned document reader; the legacy SVG preview remains available.

**Limits and pending work.** A draft lives in the tab: another tab or device does not see it, and a reload after the tab is closed starts over. Back after a reload of the review lands on the item without the focus on the control that led there (the browser loads that page anew). Where "Fortsätt granska" and a row under Dokument lead to the same step, the way back gives the focus to "Fortsätt granska". On a phone each step focuses its document heading; required documents must render before approval is enabled. The count beside Att göra among the pages changes at once, not after the pause. Not verified with a screen reader.

## Att göra

**Works in this checkout.** [D126](../../decisions/D126-one-page-per-thing-one-astryx-template-per-page-type.md) rule 2, board Att göra, Astryx's `table-inbox` template, at `/todo`, a queue that indexes a reading pane. A person with several assignments ([D135](../../decisions/D135-a-person-with-several-assignments-sees-the-work-of-all-of-them.md)) has the work of all of them in the one queue, and a row whose work item is about one of their units names it first on its line. The title says how much needs the person and waits on others; the three piles are tabs that are links (`tab`: Behöver dig, left out of the address, `waiting`, `done`): what needs the person (due, later, proposals, notices), what waits on others with what the person set aside under it (Inte nu, each with its own state), and what is done, newest first. A filter field (`filter`) and the system (`area`) in the title's row narrow the rows, and the count left is said. The rows are Astryx's `Table`: the item's name is the row's link, in semibold while it is due now, with whom it is about under it; the system as a token; when it is due as a badge, or for Klart when it was done and with what outcome. Choosing a row shows the work item in the pane beside the list and puts it in the address (`item`), at 55 % of the column and resizable (the pane at least 440 px, the list 300 px); the open row is the current one (`aria-current`) with Astryx's row status in its gutter saying so in words. The pane is the item at its narrow width, the same parts as its page: the source line, its title as the link to its page and ✕, the decisions directly under the title, which never scroll away, two opposed ones side by side at every width and the rest under "Fler åtgärder", then the facts, "Varför ser jag detta?", the rows and "Händelser". ✕ or Esc closes it and gives the focus back to the row; an item that left the pile with its own button (Avfärda, Inte nu) gives the focus to the next row. Below 900 px of column there is no pane: a row leads to the item's page with the queue as the way back, and an item in the address is replaced by its page. On a phone the pages are in the header's "Meny" and the tabs are short words. Below 768 px ([D130](../../decisions/D130-clews-look-and-layout-are-the-startsidan-prototypes-built-with-astryx.md) item 19, amended 2026-11-23) the pile is no table: each work item is one row of Astryx's `List` (`LinkRow`), the title its one link and the whole row that link's target, under it one line with the system, the unit and what the table's row says under its title, and under that where it stands in words: a plain to-do's due day as the badge Hem draws ("Försenad", "I dag", "Vecka 48"), any other state as the vocabulary's mark with its word ("Väntar · inte nu", "Klart · i dag 08.13"), nothing for a to-do with no day. Neither line is cut. The card's heading is not drawn there, since the current tab over it says the same; it stays for a screen reader with how many rows the filter left, and names the region. The filter and the system share a row from 360 px while the system's name is twelve characters or fewer, and stand one under the other otherwise. "Fråga Clew" on an item leads to a conversation about it. An item the person no longer has says so in the pane. A source not read yet is said above the rows; one that does not answer is said on its own open rows, "· kan vara gammal" at the end of the why line, and in the item's head with since when ("Kan vara gammal · Personalsystemet svarar inte sedan 14:32"); an empty pile then waits and names the system, and it is spoken once in the session ([D116, amended 2026-11-12](../../decisions/D116-a-panel-is-a-peek-at-one-thing-a-list-is-a-page-and-every-view-has-an-address.md)). Hem's tabs and area from before (`/?tab=`, `/?area=`) lead here. Att göra among the pages carries the count of what needs the person. The page is 40 px wider than the frame every other page has, so the list keeps a readable title column beside the pane.

**Limits and pending work.** Astryx's `Table` has no styled state for an open row, so the row is marked by its gutter's status and `aria-current` alone ([open questions](../../maintainers/open-questions.md)). On a phone the four key figures still stand over the queue, two by two at 375 px and one under the other at 320 px, so the first row starts below the first screen; the phone's list has not been compared with its canvases (they could not be opened when it was built) or tried signed in on the development stack. The page measures its own width with the template's `ResizeObserver` (D112 gap 4). The person's own action points are not rows here; they stay on Hem ([open questions](../../maintainers/open-questions.md)). The browser scenarios are in `tests/a11y/queue.spec.ts` and `tests/a11y/todo.spec.ts`; no signed-in pass and no screen-reader pass.

## The work item

**Works in this checkout.** A work item has one shape at two widths ([D126](../../decisions/D126-one-page-per-thing-one-astryx-template-per-page-type.md) rules 1 and 2, the `work-item-detail` template, boards Att göra and Ärende): narrow in Att göra's reading pane, beside a conversation and in a conversation's answer; as a page at `/items/<threadId>`, reached by its name in every row and its title in a pane, with `from` as the way back (named when Clew can name the page, else Att göra). The page has the way back with the source beside it, the title as its heading with when it is due, the buttons directly under the title, the read in the column and the facts under "Uppgifter" in a 340 px end panel (above the read at 1024 px and below); on a phone the next step is pinned to the foot and the rest is under ⋯. The buttons: the one next step from the connector, filled only when the item is due today (D48), with the decisions beside it, so two opposed decisions (Godkänn and Avslå) stand side by side at every width; "Fråga Clew", which leads to a conversation about the item; and ⋯ "Fler åtgärder" with the item's reads, the rest of its actions, "Inte nu" (D49) and Avfärda. Every earlier behaviour stays: one-press execute with its loading state, `confirm`, "Granska N" to the review's page, Påminn mig, Kontrollera status, Återöppna. Nothing unfolds inside the item (D126 rule 3): each of its reads is a link to the read's own page, with the item's page as the way back ([D116](../../decisions/D116-a-panel-is-a-peek-at-one-thing-a-list-is-a-page-and-every-view-has-an-address.md)), and a read out of Clew opens in a new tab and says so; an address from before that unfolded a read leads to the read's page. The column holds the receipt after a decision (each row read back with its outcome and source, Ångra with the minutes left), a notice's text (an item whose execute is the person's read receipt leads with the draft's `summary` paragraph by paragraph, under no heading, then its first read with `target: 'link'` as the way to the whole of it, marked as leaving and not repeated among the buttons; the text stays after the receipt), "Varför ser jag detta?" (for a notice the provenance line alone: the source's reason and where and when the item came), the rows as Astryx's checkbox list with how many are chosen (not drawn on any item when their one row only repeats the title, with no detail, subject or choice), "Dokument" (each row's document leads to the review's page at that row) and "Händelser": what the person and Clew did, read from the item's own audit rows by `GET /api/threads/<id>/events` (only for an item the person may see; the person is "Du", nobody else is named, no model text), with Clew's planned reminder on top. When the item leaves the person's hands with its own button (Avfärda, Inte nu), the page leads back to `from`, and the toast keeps the way back to it. An item from Clew itself names no source (`fromClewItself`). In Swedish text the item is an "ärende" (headings, labels, toasts, errors, the server's own messages). `/cards/<threadId>` and `?card=<threadId>` on any page but Att göra lead to the item's page.

**Limits and pending work.** The item carries no reference of its own (the boards' "EKO-2231") and no "your role", amount or cost centre: the protocol has no field for them, so they are not drawn. The rows' checkboxes are the selection for the action (`selectable`, `selected`); before a decision nothing is done, so the line says how many are chosen ("2 av 3 valda") rather than "klara", and no progress bar is drawn. A read the person opened is not recorded and is not in Händelser. The phone's bar holds the next step alone (the board draws two) so its words fit at 320 px. A notice's link out is the source's read with `target: 'link'`, so it is a button that asks the source for the address when pressed and opens it in a new tab, not a plain link; the notice's text is the draft's `summary`, which the protocol does not bound. The calendar's proposals keep their own card (D27). A write whose `path` asks something or has an effect opens the action dialog from every place its button is drawn ([what a write asks](protocol.md#what-a-write-asks-and-what-it-says-it-will-do)); its receipt is the work item's. While the item's system does not answer, its head says it may be old and since when, and the dialog's last step, a `confirm` and a decision on its way say that what is sent is queued and read back (D116, amended 2026-11-12); an action with an `effect` read still cannot be sent while that read fails, since the step waits for it. The item has no box of its own: "Fråga Clew" leads to the conversation about it ([The conversation](interface.md#the-conversation-and-the-thing-beside-it)). Not verified with a screen reader.

## Card actions in the browser

## Browser updates

**Works in this checkout.** Authenticated SSE invalidates the query cache using committed revisions; authorization changes clear retained state. Persisted attention budget/bundles and batch claims prevent repeated announcements across tabs. A delivery that quiet hours or the day's budget defers commits with its batch's `attention-release` job and that job's `scheduledJob` workflow, and the engine makes the batch ready at its time; the release waits for a delivery of the batch still being admitted and releases it with the rest, and a delivery admitted after the release has claimed its job is written ready.

**Limits and pending work.** At-most-once announcement can be lost after claim; the card remains. No push/email or live delivery SLO.
