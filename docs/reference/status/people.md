# Implementation status: people, units and talks

One page of [implementation status](../status.md). Each entry says what works in this checkout, what limits it or is still to do, and where the code and tests that show it are.

## Personal

**Works in this checkout.** A manager's register among Clew's pages under the header's band (D94, D130; [D126](../../decisions/D126-one-page-per-thing-one-astryx-template-per-page-type.md) rule 8, board Personal, the `table-page` template), read from the entities API (D97) and knowing no feature: the reader's reports (D99, the organisation tree), one card per unit they manage: the people assigned to the unit and, down the organisation tree, the managers of the nearest units that have one and the people on a unit nobody leads; never the people those managers lead. The talks round and the calendar's team read the same list. The title with the unit and the count under it, a search field that narrows the rows by name (`?q=`, replaced as the person types), then the views of the same people as tabs, each a link with its own address: Medarbetare (`/staff`), one tab per lens an administrator offers (`?show=<capability>`, D97 item 3: Frånvaro, Utvecklingssamtal), with how many of its rows need the reader beside its name, Kompetens (the manager's matrix, `/competence/matrix`, or a registrar's search, `/competence/search`) and Introduktion och avslut (`/boarding`), each drawn only for someone the server would answer and while its module is visible to the person (D23); a pressed tab keeps the focus on the page it leads to, and the pages' row marks Personal on every tab. Medarbetare shows the columns an administrator chose, the source named under each heading, and "Just nu" with the marks of the offered lenses and the reader's cards about the person ("1 att agera på", "N förslag"), the cards found by the person's id ([D122](../../decisions/D122-clew-keys-its-own-records-about-a-person-by-the-persons-id.md)) and a lens's marks by the source's key: one card leads to the item's page, several to the person's page at Ärenden (`/people/<personId>?section=needs-you&from=…`), each with the list as the way back. A lens's tab is that source's view sorted by need, and a name there leads to the person's page at that lens's tab. Each row's name is the link to the person's page, the one way a person is shown; its menu holds the actions about a person, grouped by system, and a source's way in under that source ("Öppna samtalet", the person's talk tab). An action opens the action dialog over the list and nothing is created before its last button; the work item it makes opens on its page with the list, its tab kept, as the way back; a dialog closed without sending gives the focus back to the row's menu button. Addresses from before keep working, replaced in the history: `?person=` and `?cards=` lead to the person's page, `?talk=<personId>` to the person's talk tab, `?card=` to the item's page. A read about the reader's people opened as a page of its own leads to its lens's tab here, and its fuller read about one person to that person's page at the tab ([D127](../../decisions/D127-a-read-can-say-its-rows-are-a-read-about-people-asked-for-everyone.md)). Where the table no longer keeps its least width (Astryx's `resolveColumnWidths`) it is drawn as its rows, and so on a phone. A source that does not answer is said once with "Försök igen"; the rest stands. Someone who manages nobody gets the page's empty state.

**Limits and pending work.** No notes. A person who reports from a unit below is listed like the people assigned to the unit: the page does not say which unit they lead, which the earlier role column did ("Chef för …"). Managers of the same unit are listed with each other. "1 att agera på" beside "1 förslag" leads to a list of one card: the list's read names a card only when the person has exactly one in all (`attention.threadId`). Only the development stack's synthetic directory has filled it, and the page has not been looked at signed in as a manager with a report from below since D126. A manual screen-reader pass remains open.

## Utvecklingssamtal (talks)

**Works in this checkout.** Clew's first owned domain (D96), on synthetic data: `modules/talks` with its own tables (migrations 0043 and 0080). An operator opens a round with `scripts/talk-round.ts`. Per person on a manager's units the talk is missing, booked or held (a manager marks it, audited, with undo). When a talk is booked is the talk's own record ([D132](../../decisions/D132-when-a-talk-is-booked-is-the-talks-own-record-and-the-calendar-holds-a-copy-of-the-invitation.md)): the time, the manager whose booking it is and the invitation that carries it are stored on the talk, and nothing is read from a calendar or recognised by a meeting's title. An invitation Clew sent books the talk when it has been read back from the calendar by its own id, and its undo, its replacement and the invitee's no end the booking, each in the transaction that changes the invitation (the calendar module's `invitations` port, bound to the talks module in composition). A manager registers a time agreed on by hand on the talk's tab (Ange tid, Ändra tid, Ta bort tiden; `PUT` and `DELETE /api/talks/:personId/booking`), with nothing sent; that is refused for a talk marked held and while an invitation carries the booking. A booking counts while the manager whose booking it is still manages the person, read when asked, so a reorganisation writes nothing here and the person's managers then see a talk that lacks a time. Every current manager of the person reads the booking with its time. A talk follows its meeting by the event's id ([D133](../../decisions/D133-a-talk-follows-its-meeting-by-the-events-id-and-only-the-manager-changes-it.md)): the calendar module reads the event of each invitation back by the ref it stored, as part of the manager's scan and at most once an hour, and tells the talks module what the calendar holds. When the meeting is at another time or gone, that is recorded beside the booking, which stands; the talk's tab says both, the meeting in the calendar and the talk in Clew, and the manager whose booking it is settles it there (`POST /api/talks/:personId/booking/calendar`): move the talk to the meeting's time, or, for a meeting that is gone, take the time off or keep it as a time set by hand. That manager also gets a card the talks connector's scan derives from the talk each time, which names nobody, opens the person's place in the round and is resolved when the difference is gone. A meeting moved or cancelled on Clew's calendar page is found by its event's ref and moves or ends the invitation and the talk in the same transaction, with no second decision. Two more cards hang on the booked time, derived by the same scan and stored nowhere ([D134](../../decisions/D134-a-booked-talk-reminds-its-manager-to-prepare-and-asks-afterwards-whether-it-was-held.md)): "Förbered utvecklingssamtalet" from the round's number of working days before the talk (`talk_round.prepare_days`, five unless the operator says otherwise with `--prepare-days`, 0 for none) until it starts, and "Hölls utvecklingssamtalet?" from the day after its time while it is not marked held. The talk's tab then says the time has passed: Markera som hållet marks it held on the day it was booked for, and Samtalet blev inte av takes the time off, by removing a time set by hand or by ending the invitation (`missed`), so the talk is booked again. How the parts hang together, the talk, the invitation and the meeting, is explained in [Development talks](../../architecture/talks.md). The module says so through two reads about an entity (D97): `talks.person`, a section on the opened person and, where an administrator chose it, a lens, a column ("Nästa samtal") and marks on Personal, and `talks.unit`, a section on the unit page. One person's talk is the talks source's tab on their page ([D126](../../decisions/D126-one-page-per-thing-one-astryx-template-per-page-type.md) rule 3), reached by the tab and by "Öppna samtalet" in Personal's row menu; an address that laid it over a page (`?talk=<personId>`, a cold `/staff?talk=<personId>` included) leads to that tab, replaced in the history. The planner takes its deadline and team from the round and the assignments. A person has one talk a round, whichever of their current managers books it ([D96, talks](../../decisions/D96-talks-is-the-first-owned-domain-the-round-and-action-points-behind-a-profile.md), as amended): the planner reads who is held and who is booked from the round (`PlanningRound.booked`), so the reads, the round's status and the nightly card's count show the person as booked and the planner leaves them out. Beside the round stand Clew's own proposals, which hold a person where another system answers the round. A confirmation of a time for such a person, or for one whose talk is held, is refused and names the person, never the time or the other manager; for a time the round records that no other manager's invitation carries, with "… har redan ett utvecklingssamtal bokat.". So is a confirmation while another manager's approved invitation to the same person may still be sent (running, pending (even with a blocking reason), or unknown, inside the approval's 24 hours and the round's period, even after the approving manager leaves the team, since they can regain the employee and retry before expiry), with "… bjuds redan in till ett utvecklingssamtal av en annan chef."; an invitation that Clew's own check stopped right before sending, because the time or the room was not free or could not be read, says "Inget skickades: …", settles failed and holds nobody. While the round cannot be read, a confirmation is refused with "Det gick inte att se om … redan har ett utvecklingssamtal bokat. Försök igen om en stund." and the planner plans nothing. The dispatch skips an invitation to a person booked meanwhile inside the round the confirmation was approved in, which it does not read again, or when it cannot tell. The scan keeps an open card that has no decision on its way in step with its count, in one write with the audit row `proposals.settled_elsewhere`: a drawn time of a person another manager invited, for whom the round records a time set by hand ("har redan en tid bokad"), or whose talk is held, becomes a row with that reason; a row of someone the manager's own sent invitation holds says "har ett samtal i din kalender"; a row of someone who lacks a time again, or of someone of the team the card leaves out, says "behöver en tid"; the row of someone who left the team leaves the card; and the card's "N samtal saknar tid" is counted again. Every scan reads the round, the team and the invitations, all Clew's own records, and no calendar, so what changed reaches the card at the next scan. Any current manager may mark the talk held, move its day or undo it; the audit row names who. A proposal names its invitee by the person's id beside the address it was drawn with (migration 0056), so a changed work address keeps the booking, the plan and the reminders. A manager adds action points for either owner; each puts a card without text in its owner's stream with a `due` timer for the morning after the date; the owner reads and ticks them off under Mina åtgärdspunkter on Hem, with Ångra; open ones carry over to the next round. A point is ticked off by its owner; a current manager of the employee may take over a manager's point whose owner no longer manages the employee or was erased, and the talk's tab names that point's owner "Tidigare chef". A co-manager's points and the employee's own stay with their owners, a former manager's own points stay on that manager's Hem until they are done, and `talks.commitment_done` and `talks.commitment_reopened` say whether the owner or a manager acted. The talks seam compares a source's person keys in NFC and without regard to case, so a talks system that writes `Anna.Berg` for `anna.berg` keeps its rows. The text is sealed at rest and is in no audit row, card or model context. The figure "Utvecklingssamtal hållna" belongs to the domain's area. The profile reads `<id>.talks` and `<id>.commitments` are in the protocol; the pages read the composites `clew.talks` and `clew.commitments`; one system answers per installation: the bundled `talks` connector while enabled, else the single connector declaring the profile, shown read-only (`connectors/appraisals` is the fixture, registered as a draft). `scripts/talks-export.ts` and `scripts/talks-erase.ts` export the domain and erase one person, named by their id; an id the people table does not hold and a person who is still active are refused.

**Limits and pending work.** No real data until checklist questions 6 and 11 are answered (D96). Not built: the record (preparation, notes, approval), templates, an HR view, a talk without a round, a page for opening a round, an employee adding a point, sending an invitation to one person from their row (the calendar has no action about a person; a time agreed on is registered by hand), the employee's own preparation and anything telling the employee beyond the calendar invitation. A working day is Monday to Friday with no holiday calendar, a reminder or a question appears at the manager's next scan (the nightly one or their first visit), and the question whether a talk was held stays until it is answered or the round is closed. The time of a booking an invitation carries is not changed on the talk itself: the manager moves the meeting, on Clew's calendar page or in the calendar, and the talk follows (D133). Only the meeting's time and that it exists are read back, not who is invited or its room, and no real CalDAV or Microsoft 365 calendar has been used for it. A meeting changed in the calendar reaches the talk at the next hourly reading, which each server process keeps for itself and which the scan starts without waiting for, and the card can lag the talk's tab by one scan. A card about a person without a user name opens the round and not the person. A talk booked by hand in a calendar counts only once its manager registers the time. An invitation read back while no round is open books nothing. A booking belongs to the round open when it was recorded, whatever its day, while the check on other managers' proposals is bounded by the round's period; a new round takes over the talks that are booked for a time still to come. A time set by hand between the approval of an invitation and its sending is replaced by the invitation. By decision a manager never ticks off the employee's own point or a co-manager's (D96, talks, as amended). An approved invitation whose outcome is unknown holds the person for their other managers for its 24 hours; after them it counts only once a check reads it back. The card's "N samtal saknar tid" is counted again when the scan changes a row or a drawn time and on another week, not when the manager confirms times of their own. A drawn time stays on the card for someone who has left the team, whose confirmation is refused with "Deltagarna måste tillhöra din enhet.". A talks system behind a connector names a person by user name, the key on the wire ([D122](../../decisions/D122-clew-keys-its-own-records-about-a-person-by-the-persons-id.md)): after a rename it answers for the person only once it has the new name, and a reused name reaches its current holder; Clew's own talks tables key the employee by id. Switching the built-in connector off hides its cards until it is on again. The planner's round read has the composite's four-second budget, and a person with the manager role who manages no unit causes a logged error on each read. The browser tells "no talks system" from a failure by the refusal's sentence. The section on Hem is not reserved while it loads. `--if-none` on the round script checks and opens in two transactions. No signed-in pass on the development stack and no screen-reader pass yet, the form for the time included.

## Kompetens (competence)

**Works in this checkout.** Proposed in D108 and built against the freeze on new surface (D101, the first real integration); it was brought into `main` on 2026-11-11 without the product owner's word on the freeze, which the product owner lifted on 2026-11-12, so it stays; it is switched off until an administrator enables it. A second owned domain, on synthetic data: `modules/competence` with its own tables (migration 0049). One record, a holding, for a competence and a certificate, typed against a catalogue an administrator with `admin.competence` keeps on Administration › Kompetens (add, change, archive; a new validity says what it does to the holdings before it is saved), whose section Administration lists only while the module is switched on and the person holds `admin.competence` (2026-11-12); an address from before while it is off gets the page's own answer. The manager a person reports to (D99) registers a certificate in a dialog over the page they are on: Personal at `/staff?register=<personId>`, or, since 2026-11-13, the person's own page (`/people/<key>?register=<personId>`), since the section's way in is a query alone (`?register={id}`); closing it replaces its address, so Back never opens it again. Where a holding stands (valid, expiring within 90 days, expired) is computed from the dates when read, never stored. The bundled `competence` connector has `competence.attention` and `competence.holdings` with declared views, `competence.person`, a read about a person (D97) whose section's page is the registration form, an area with a figure on Hem, and a scan that gives a person a card per holding that has run out or soon will and a manager one card that counts and names nobody. Its row is created disabled: an administrator switches it on under Anslutna system, and until then no area, read, card or route answers. `scripts/competence-export.ts` and `scripts/competence-erase.ts` export the domain (`clew.competence/2` since 2026-11-12) and erase one person, named by their id, with the cards the competence scan put in their stream (each with a `thread.removed` row); an id the people table does not hold and a person who is still active are refused. `scripts/competence-seed.ts` seeds the development organisation.

What a source holds is copied in (D108 item 9, 2026-11-12, migration 0061). Every system that answers the holdings profile is read once a day at 04:00 and when an administrator presses "Läs in nu", by the durable workflow `competenceCopy`, never two runs of one source at once; each read is one transaction with one audit row of counts. A key is matched to an active person by user name, a key that matches nobody is counted and never stored, and a repeated read changes only when the rows were read. A row is revoked for absence only after a complete read of a source that says it reports withdrawals. A page the source does not answer is asked again twice, a refused cursor starts the sweep again once, and any other failure ends the read, which keeps what it read and is recorded with the failure's kind. A copied row whose type an administrator has mapped counts in the status, the reads, the figure and the cards exactly as a registered one; one whose type is not mapped is listed on a person's holdings under the source's label as "räknas inte" and counts towards nothing. Every listed copied row shows its source and when it was read. Administration › Kompetens › Källor (`/admin/competence/sources`) shows each source's last read and how it went, starts a read, and maps each of its types to the catalogue in a dialog with an address, re-typing what is already copied. Removing a connector removes what it put in the register; `scripts/competence-forget-source.ts` does it for one that stays, `scripts/competence-read.ts` starts a read and prints its counts, and `scripts/competence-map.ts` maps a type (with `--development`, the development learning platform's seeded courses).

Requirements, coverage and share targets (D108 items 4 to 6, 2026-11-12, migrations 0062 and 0063). A person's positions arrive over SCIM as `position:` groups (see [identity](identity.md)). A requirement names a type and one of a position, a unit or a person; an administrator with `admin.competence` sets the first two on Administration › Kompetens › Krav (`/admin/competence/requirements`), where share targets are kept too, and the manager a person reports to sets one for that person alone (a route; no page yet), which the administrator never sees ([D118](../../decisions/D118-an-administrators-capability-is-no-relation-to-a-person.md)). A required type a person has no counting holding of is `missing` ("Saknas"), computed when read like the other statuses, and it counts with what has run out in the person's section, on Personal, in the figure, in `competence.attention` and in the cards; a person's words are, in order, Utgånget, Saknas, Går ut snart, Giltigt, Inget registrerat. `competence.unit` is a read about a unit for the manager who leads it: coverage per required type and each share target's share, for the reader's own reports only, however many others the branch holds, since a row that mixed the two could be subtracted from another to single a person out. People the reader does not manage are counted only in a share target, which is measured once, over what it was set on: a unit's on that unit's read for the manager who leads it, an organisation-wide one on the Krav page as a figure that names nobody. A target is shown when the people in it whom the reader does not manage are none or at least five, and withheld ("För få för att visas") otherwise, as are two targets of one type whose positions overlap. A requirement and a share target name a position by its code and a unit by its id (migration 0057), so they outlive a position group that is removed and pushed again and can name a unit nobody is placed on; one whose position or unit is gone is listed as "Finns inte längre" and counts towards nothing. A card closes when its cause is gone and comes back when the cause does: the scan reports a complete observation, a person's card follows the person and the type (not a holding's row, so a source read again does not re-issue it), and its identity changes with the end date, the requirements that apply and the last revocation; the manager's card is one occurrence that comes back when its counts change, up or down. A position that disappears and returns with the same code brings the same identity, so that card does not reopen. Cards stored by the scan as it was before are retired once by an operator with `scripts/competence-cards-reset.ts`.

The Kompetens place (D120 item 8, 2026-11-12) is no longer a place of its own ([D126](../../decisions/D126-one-page-per-thing-one-astryx-template-per-page-type.md) rule 8): "Mina kompetenser", the reader's own holdings and what is required of them, drawn from `competence.holdings`, is the Kompetens tab of the reader's own page, where `/competence` leads with its dialogs kept; Personal's Kompetens tab is the matrix for a manager and the search for a registrar. `/competence/matrix` is a manager's reports against the types required of them (D108 item 11), not redrawn in D126 and waiting for a real grid ([open questions](../../maintainers/open-questions.md)), a first-party page on Astryx's `table-comparison`: at most thirty types at a time (ten until 2026-11-13), the set chosen by area or position and kept in the address, a list of people where the table does not fit. From a cell the manager registers a certificate with the type chosen, revokes a holding that was registered in Clew and removes a requirement set for that one person; "Lägg till krav för en person" in the page's header sets one. The matrix downloads as CSV, audited as `competence.matrix_exported` with counts. Since the interface round of 2026-11-13: a cell is one control that shows one word (a badge for Att granska, Saknas, Utgånget and Går ut snart, plain text otherwise) and opens a menu whose title holds the details and whose rows are the actions, so there is no separate menu button per cell and six columns fit wholly at 1280 px where four did; the set can be narrowed by area or position also when everything fits (`by`), and to what needs attention (`only=attention`, filtered on the page); in the competence lists the name in a row opens the row (the catalogue's type, a source's mapping, a skill's level), and the columns that held "Ändra" and "Koppla" are gone. Rows with nothing to open: a requirement and a waiting or rejected registration of one's own. The gap in Astryx (D120) showed more with narrow columns: tabbing backwards through twenty columns at 1280 px, 8 of 19 stops rested under the pinned names. A person's own registration (D108 item 7, 2026-11-13, migration 0065). "Lägg till" on `/competence` (`?add=new`) registers a certificate for the reader. A manager's count card is a new card when the set of things due changes, and a person's "saknas" card is dated by the earliest requirement that applies, so removing the earliest of two still brings it back.

Skills with levels (D120 items 1 to 6, 2026-11-13, migration 0066). `skill` ("Färdighet") is a kind in the catalogue; a holding of such a type carries a level, Grundläggande, Självständig or Expert, and no end date, one per person and type. A person rates their own on `/competence` with the mark "Vill utveckla" if they wish; it is listed at once as "Egen bedömning" to them and to a manager they report to, and opens no work item. A requirement on a skill names a least level, and only a confirmed skill at or above it meets the requirement; otherwise the type is missing and the level held is shown beside it.
