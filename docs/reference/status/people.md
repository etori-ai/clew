# Implementation status: people, units and talks

One page of [implementation status](../status.md). Each entry says what works in this checkout, what limits it or is still to do, and where the code and tests that show it are.

## Personal

**Works in this checkout.** A manager's place in the rail, after Kalender (D94, D112 item 1), read from the entities API (D97) and knowing no feature: the reader's reports (D99, the organisation tree), one card per unit they manage: the people assigned to the unit and, down the organisation tree, the managers of the nearest units that have one and the people on a unit nobody leads; never the people those managers lead. The talks round and the calendar's team read the same list. "Alla" shows the columns an administrator chose, the source named under each heading, and "Just nu" with the marks of the offered lenses and the reader's cards about the person ("1 att agera på", "N förslag"): one card opens beside the list (`?card=`), several lead to the person's page at Behöver dig (`/people/<key>?section=needs-you&from=…`; an old `?cards=` address leads there), D111 amended. "Visa" chooses a lens, kept in the address as `?show=`, and the list becomes that source's view sorted by need. Each row has a menu of the actions about a person, grouped by system; an action's card opens beside the list, with the lens kept, and closing it gives the focus back to the row's menu button. A name leads to the person's page, the one way a person is shown (D111 rule 3); an address that drew a person beside the list (`?person=`) leads there. The panel stands beside the list from 1336 px, as on every page, and the list gives way: where the table no longer keeps its least width beside the panel (Astryx's `resolveColumnWidths`; 1020 px for the development stack's two columns, so below a 1780 px window) it is drawn as its rows; below 1336 px the panel is the drawer over it. Personal never gives way to Hem for a card. A source that does not answer is said once with "Försök igen"; the rest stands. Someone who manages nobody gets the page's empty state.

**Limits and pending work.** The hard-wired talk columns are gone; the talk is reached from the talks section's "Öppna samtalet", and its drawer still reads `GET /api/staff`, which answers 403 to anyone who manages no unit. No notes. A person who reports from a unit below is listed like the people assigned to the unit: the page does not say which unit they lead, which the earlier role column did ("Chef för …"), and the browser scenario for it left with that column. Managers of the same unit are listed with each other. With "Alla"'s column weights the table needs 1020 px, so on most laptop windows a card is the drawer over Personal rather than beside it. "1 att agera på" beside "1 förslag" leads to a list of one card: the list's read names a card only when the person has exactly one in all (`attention.threadId`). Only the development stack's synthetic directory has filled it, and the page has not been looked at signed in as a manager with a report from below. No signed-in pass on the development stack with the new list and no screen-reader pass.

## Utvecklingssamtal (talks)

**Works in this checkout.** Clew's first owned domain (D96), on synthetic data: `modules/talks` with its own tables (migration 0043). An operator opens a round with `scripts/talk-round.ts`. Per person on a manager's units the talk is missing, booked (read from the calendar) or held (the manager marks it, audited, with undo). The module says so through two reads about an entity (D97): `talks.person`, a section on the opened person and, where an administrator chose it, a lens, a column ("Nästa samtal") and marks on Personal, and `talks.unit`, a section on the unit page. One person's talk is a drawer over the page its "Öppna samtalet" is on, Personal's row menu or the talks section on the person's page, at that page's address with `talk=<personId>` added; closing it leaves the page as it was, and a cold `/staff?talk=<personId>` is Personal with the talk over it (D111 rule 5). The planner takes its deadline and team from the round and the assignments. A manager adds action points for either owner; each puts a card without text in its owner's stream with a `due` timer for the morning after the date; the owner reads and ticks them off under Mina åtgärdspunkter on Hem, with Ångra; open ones carry over to the next round. The text is sealed at rest and is in no audit row, card or model context. The figure "Utvecklingssamtal hållna" belongs to the domain's area. The profile reads `<id>.talks` and `<id>.commitments` are in the protocol; the pages read the composites `clew.talks` and `clew.commitments`; one system answers per installation: the bundled `talks` connector while enabled, else the single connector declaring the profile, shown read-only (`connectors/appraisals` is the fixture, registered as a draft). `scripts/talks-export.ts` and `scripts/talks-erase.ts` export the domain and erase one person.

**Limits and pending work.** No real data until checklist questions 6 and 11 are answered (D96). Not built: the record (preparation, notes, approval), templates, an HR view, a talk without a round, a page for opening a round, an employee adding a point, a manager ticking off an employee's point, booking one person's talk (the calendar has no action about a person). A system behind a connector is told the caller's directory user name in the signed actor (D97); whether that stays the person's key is open. Switching the built-in connector off hides its cards until it is on again. The planner's round read shares a four-second budget with a calendar read, and a person with the manager role who manages no unit causes a logged error on each read. The browser tells "no talks system" from a failure by the refusal's sentence. The section on Hem is not reserved while it loads. `--if-none` on the round script checks and opens in two transactions. No signed-in pass on the development stack and no screen-reader pass yet.

## Kompetens (competence)

**Works in this checkout.** Proposed in D108 and built against the freeze on new surface (D101, the first real integration); it was brought into `main` on 2026-11-11 without the product owner's word on the freeze, which the product owner lifted on 2026-11-12, so it stays; it is switched off until an administrator enables it. A second owned domain, on synthetic data: `modules/competence` with its own tables (migration 0049). One record, a holding, for a competence and a certificate, typed against a catalogue an administrator with `admin.competence` keeps on Administration › Kompetens (add, change, archive; a new validity says what it does to the holdings before it is saved), whose row the rail draws only while the module is switched on and the person holds `admin.competence` (2026-11-12); an address from before while it is off gets the page's own answer. The manager a person reports to (D99) registers a certificate in a dialog over Personal at `/staff?register=<personId>`. Where a holding stands (valid, expiring within 90 days, expired) is computed from the dates when read, never stored. The bundled `competence` connector has `competence.attention` and `competence.holdings` with declared views, `competence.person`, a read about a person (D97) whose section's page is the registration form, an area with a figure on Hem, and a scan that gives a person a card per holding that has run out or soon will and a manager one card that counts and names nobody. Its row is created disabled: an administrator switches it on under Anslutna system, and until then no area, read, card or route answers. `scripts/competence-export.ts` and `scripts/competence-erase.ts` export the domain (`clew.competence/2` since 2026-11-12) and erase one person; `scripts/competence-seed.ts` seeds the development organisation.

What a source holds is copied in (D108 item 9, 2026-11-12, migration 0054). Every system that answers the holdings profile is read once a day at 04:00 and when an administrator presses "Läs in nu", by the durable workflow `competenceCopy`, never two runs of one source at once; each read is one transaction with one audit row of counts. A key is matched to an active person by user name, a key that matches nobody is counted and never stored, and a repeated read changes only when the rows were read. A row is revoked for absence only after a complete read of a source that says it reports withdrawals. A page the source does not answer is asked again twice, a refused cursor starts the sweep again once, and any other failure ends the read, which keeps what it read and is recorded with the failure's kind. A copied row whose type an administrator has mapped counts in the status, the reads, the figure and the cards exactly as a registered one; one whose type is not mapped is listed on a person's holdings under the source's label as "räknas inte" and counts towards nothing. Every listed copied row shows its source and when it was read. Administration › Kompetens › Källor (`/admin/competence/sources`) shows each source's last read and how it went, starts a read, and maps each of its types to the catalogue in a dialog with an address, re-typing what is already copied. Removing a connector removes what it put in the register; `scripts/competence-forget-source.ts` does it for one that stays, `scripts/competence-read.ts` starts a read and prints its counts, and `scripts/competence-map.ts` maps a type (with `--development`, the development learning platform's seeded courses).

Requirements, coverage and share targets (D108 items 4 to 6, 2026-11-12, migrations 0055 and 0056). A person's positions arrive over SCIM as `position:` groups (see [identity](identity.md)). A requirement names a type and one of a position, a unit or a person; an administrator with `admin.competence` sets the first two on Administration › Kompetens › Krav (`/admin/competence/requirements`), where share targets are kept too, and the manager a person reports to sets one for that person alone (a route; no page yet), which the administrator never sees (D118). A required type a person has no counting holding of is `missing` ("Saknas"), computed when read like the other statuses, and it counts with what has run out in the person's section, on Personal, in the figure, in `competence.attention` and in the cards; a person's words are, in order, Utgånget, Saknas, Går ut snart, Giltigt, Inget registrerat. `competence.unit` is a read about a unit for the manager who leads it: coverage per required type and each share target's share, for the reader's own reports only, however many others the branch holds, since a row that mixed the two could be subtracted from another to single a person out. People the reader does not manage are counted only in a share target, which is measured once, over what it was set on: a unit's on that unit's read for the manager who leads it, an organisation-wide one on the Krav page as a figure that names nobody. A target is shown when the people in it whom the reader does not manage are none or at least five, and withheld ("För få för att visas") otherwise, as are two targets of one type whose positions overlap. A requirement and a share target name a position by its code and a unit by its id (migration 0057), so they outlive a position group that is removed and pushed again and can name a unit nobody is placed on; one whose position or unit is gone is listed as "Finns inte längre" and counts towards nothing. A card closes when its cause is gone and comes back when the cause does: the scan reports a complete observation, a person's card follows the person and the type (not a holding's row, so a source read again does not re-issue it), and its identity changes with the end date, the requirements that apply and the last revocation; the manager's card is one occurrence that comes back when its counts change, up or down. A position that disappears and returns with the same code brings the same identity, so that card does not reopen. Cards stored by the scan as it was before are retired once by an operator with `scripts/competence-cards-reset.ts`. The export is `clew.competence/4`.

The Kompetens place (D120 item 8, 2026-11-12): a row in the rail for everyone while the module is switched on for them. `/competence` is "Mina kompetenser", the reader's own holdings and what is required of them, drawn from `competence.holdings`. `/competence/matrix` is a manager's reports against the types required of them (D108 item 11), a first-party page on Astryx's `table-comparison`: at most ten types at a time, the set chosen by area or position and kept in the address, a list of people where the table does not fit. From a cell the manager registers a certificate with the type chosen, revokes a holding that was registered in Clew and removes a requirement set for that one person; "Lägg till krav för en person" in the page's header sets one. The matrix downloads as CSV, audited as `competence.matrix_exported` with counts.

**Limits and pending work.** No real data until checklist questions 6 and 11 are answered (D108). The copy reads the installation's read of the holdings profile (D119) through the registry; it has been run end to end in process against the development Moodle on a memory store, not yet by the running server, and `connectors/` has no fixture for the profile yet. Run again after the review's fixes, with the connector now leaving Moodle's guest account out: one page of 16 accounts, 50 holdings stored for 14 people, the administrator and the service account matching nobody, outcome `complete`. A copied row is kept only for an active person inside both the source's audience and the module's (D23), and deleted at the next read for anyone else, a deactivated person included, where item 14 keeps a registered holding for a period (chosen in the working session of 2026-11-12, waiting for the product owner's word; a provisioner fault before the daily read deletes copied rows, which come back as new rows only if the source still holds them); Källor shows a source, with that organisation's counts, only to an organisation with someone in both. Erasure refuses an active person. A person's row on Personal, the figure and the cards count copied rows without saying how old they are, and nothing marks a source not read for days. A requirement's least level is stored and not yet used (D120 item 4). The matrix's cells are separate tab stops, not an arrow-key grid. Neither Kompetens page has been seen signed in. Not built: a person's own registration and its approval; skills with levels, the catalogue import, the registrar's search (D120, D121); merging two types and bringing an archived one back; the erasure timer after deactivation (an operator runs the script); the read log. The manager's card is refreshed while open and comes again after it is handled for as long as something is due. Nobody has offered `competence.person` as a lens or a column on the development stack. ## Sources about an entity

**Works in this checkout.** A capability may say its rows are `about` a person or a unit (D97); the loader holds a read to `{ keys }`, one row per key carrying `key`, a table or list view and `model: false`, and an action to the key as its only required input. The core `entities` module checks who may be asked about (for a person: the reader, their direct reports under D99 and other active people of their organisation; for a unit: one they manage or belong to), sends keys in calls of at most 200 under a four-second budget per source with cancellation, checks authority again after the batch and stores nothing (`GET /api/entities/:kind`, `/rows`, `/:key`, `/:key/section`). An administrator offers a read as a lens and chooses at most three columns per kind on the connector's page, audited as `connector.lens` and `connector.column`. Sources: `talks.person`, `talks.unit`, `hr.person`, `appraisals.person`, `competence.person`, `qlik.unit`. The signed actor carries the caller's `userName`. `connector-check` probes the batch, an unknown key and keys out of scope, with `--keys` and `--as` for real people.

Since D117 the rule holds on every call, not only behind the entity routes: the registry resolves every key an input names (`keys`, `about.param`, `person.param`, the `person` of a `<id>.commitments` read, a property a section's row opens another read with, an execute's effect read) before any connector runs, on `/api/cap`, `/api/connectors/call`, `clew.call`, cards, tools, jobs, a document's bytes and an approved write's dispatch. `managed` is D99's direct reports only: a co-manager of the reader's unit is a colleague unless their line runs to a unit above it that the reader leads too, and anyone who manages the reader is a colleague, though Personal lists them. An approved write's dispatch redeems the resolution its check made just before the dispatch is recorded. Telling the source and binding the request are protocol 3, a break; a connector on 2 is loaded for a window. A unit is `manages` or `member`. A capability declares `person.admits` (`self` and `managed` when it does not) or `unit.admits` (`manages`); a key outside it is left out of a batch, a single one (or anything but a string) refuses the call as an unknown capability is refused, and the source is told each key's relation (`clew.about`, or `about` in the request body, which the assertion binds by its digest, for a remote connector). The loader refuses an input that names someone and is not a plain string property. Declared: `hr.person`, `hr.prepareAbsence`, `appraisals.person`, `talks.person`, `competence.person` admit `managed`; `appraisals.commitments`, `competence.holdings`, `clew.commitments` take `person` and admit `self` and `managed`; `talks.commitments` takes `person` and admits `managed`; `talks.commitments`, `clew.commitments` and `competence.holdings` take a person without a user name by Clew's id; `qlik.unit` and `talks.unit` admit `manages`. A colleague's opened page asks only sources that admit `colleague`, none in the repository does, and offers only actions that admit the reader's relation; the list's rows offer only actions that admit `managed`. An approved write is resolved again at dispatch, not when it is read; a directory that cannot be read leaves it unsent as `source-unavailable`. `connector-check` lists what each admits and vouches for no key without `--as`.

**Limits and pending work.** Entity kinds are person and unit only: no source for Kompetenser, Introduktion, access cards or cases, and no rooms. No connector reads the relation in the body yet; each still narrows on its own as well (hr by its own unit, appraisals by manager, qlik by unit, talks and competence by the people Personal lists), and that narrowing could go once the relation is trusted. Talks and competence count a co-manager among a manager's people and the core does not, so on Personal a co-manager's row gets no source's column or mark and the talk drawer reads no action points for one. A colleague named by Clew's id is not resolved. The cost of resolving the relation on every call is not measured. The person's key on the wire is the user name, provisionally. Never a model tool; no two-source list as an assistant answer. No page lists units, so a unit read is a section only. The column limit is counted without a lock. The conformance check warns above 1.5 seconds while the module allows four.

## The opened person and the unit page

**Works in this checkout.** A person is a page (`/people/<key>`, D111 rule 3), reached from a name on Personal or any row that names them; nothing draws a person beside a list, and `/staff?person=<key>` redirects to the page with the list as `from`, `person-section` as `section` and an open card kept. Since D112 item 5 (board Person) the page has the work item's page shape (`work-item-detail`). The main column holds "Behöver dig" (the reader's work items about them, drawn as the work item's own row and opening beside the page) and then every source that has something about them, one under the other with a rule between them, each with its heading, its source's name as a badge (Clew for Clew's own), its row, its fuller read and its own way in ("Öppna samtalet"), each read on its own. The details rail (340 px, "Detaljer") holds what the directory says that the page has (title, user name, "Chef: Du" for the reader's own people) under "Katalogen", and "Tillhör" with their unit, a link for a unit the reader may open; at 1024 px and below, and while the panel holds something beside the page, it is behind "Visa detaljer" in a fullscreen dialog. ## Actions about a person

**Works in this checkout.** `POST /api/entities/:kind/:key/actions` starts an action from the entity's menu without the model: the key goes into its `param`, a propose runs as the person and its draft is the card, an execute becomes a card whose button is the human decision. ## The assistant asked about a person

**Works in this checkout.** [D115](../../decisions/D115-the-assistant-resolves-a-person-the-asker-names-and-hands-the-model-the-work-about-them-not-health-rows.md). A question that names one of the people the asker manages ("Anna har varit sjuk i två veckor, vad gör jag?") is resolved by Clew among them alone: a whole name in any case, or a name written with a capital, by full name, first name or family name. With a model, the prompt says who the name is and lists the asker's open work about them (cards whose rows name them) by title, source, last day and buttons; the model may look a name up itself with `clew.findPerson`, only among people the asker's own words in the conversation could name, and reads the routine through the HR fixture's `hr.rehabRoutine`. A card about anyone the conversation names is handed by its title, last day and buttons alone, chipped or not, in that turn and the later ones; a read about the person never reaches the model. The work items are drawn in the turn; with one person and one item, the model may draft its action and "Granska och …" opens the action's dialog. Without a model, the router answers with the work item under Behöver dig and draws it, asks which when the name is several people, and otherwise goes on as before; its figure rule answers only questions about the figures, never one that asks what to do or names someone. Several matches are named; nobody is said to be nobody. The turn's audit row lists the tools and the cards, never a name.

**Limits and pending work.** No model has run it outside the scripted tests: the development stack has no model configured, so the router answers there, and Clew learns of `hr.rehabRoutine` only once the HR connector's manifest is fetched again on Anslutna system. A work item's title is handed as the source wrote it, and `hr.whoIsOn` still hands a manager's model the names of the absent with the kind of absence (both in the open questions). A pronoun in a later turn does not carry the person over. People on other units are not resolved. A model marked as safe for health data is the intended next step and is not built. The interface's status line that a conversation about a person tells the model nothing still holds for "Fråga Clew" beside a person's page, which sends no chip: the model learns who it is only when the asker writes the name.
