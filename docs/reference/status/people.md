# Implementation status: people, units and talks

One page of [implementation status](../status.md). Each entry says what works in this checkout, what limits it or is still to do, and where the code and tests that show it are.

## Personal

**Works in this checkout.** A manager's place in the rail, after Kalender (D94, D112 item 1), read from the entities API (D97) and knowing no feature: the reader's reports (D99, the organisation tree), one card per unit they manage: the people assigned to the unit and, down the organisation tree, the managers of the nearest units that have one and the people on a unit nobody leads; never the people those managers lead. The talks round and the calendar's team read the same list. "Alla" shows the columns an administrator chose, the source named under each heading, and "Just nu" with the marks of the offered lenses and the reader's cards about the person ("1 att agera på", "N förslag"): one card opens beside the list (`?card=`), several lead to the person's page at Behöver dig (`/people/<key>?section=needs-you&from=…`; an old `?cards=` address leads there), D111 amended. "Visa" chooses a lens, kept in the address as `?show=`, and the list becomes that source's view sorted by need. Each row has a menu of the actions about a person, grouped by system; an action's card opens beside the list, with the lens kept, and closing it gives the focus back to the row's menu button. A name leads to the person's page, the one way a person is shown (D111 rule 3); an address that drew a person beside the list (`?person=`) leads there. The panel stands beside the list from 1336 px, as on every page, and the list gives way: where the table no longer keeps its least width beside the panel (Astryx's `resolveColumnWidths`; 1020 px for the development stack's two columns, so below a 1780 px window) it is drawn as its rows; below 1336 px the panel is the drawer over it. Personal never gives way to Hem for a card. A source that does not answer is said once with "Försök igen"; the rest stands. Someone who manages nobody gets the page's empty state.

**Limits and pending work.** The hard-wired talk columns are gone; the talk is reached from the talks section's "Öppna samtalet", and its drawer still reads `GET /api/staff`, which answers 403 to anyone who manages no unit. No notes. A person who reports from a unit below is listed like the people assigned to the unit: the page does not say which unit they lead, which the earlier role column did ("Chef för …"), and the browser scenario for it left with that column. Managers of the same unit are listed with each other. With "Alla"'s column weights the table needs 1020 px, so on most laptop windows a card is the drawer over Personal rather than beside it. "1 att agera på" beside "1 förslag" leads to a list of one card: the list's read names a card only when the person has exactly one in all (`attention.threadId`). Only the development stack's synthetic directory has filled it, and the page has not been looked at signed in as a manager with a report from below. No signed-in pass on the development stack with the new list and no screen-reader pass.

## Utvecklingssamtal (talks)

**Works in this checkout.** Clew's first owned domain (D96), on synthetic data: `modules/talks` with its own tables (migration 0043). An operator opens a round with `scripts/talk-round.ts`. Per person on a manager's units the talk is missing, booked (read from the calendar) or held (the manager marks it, audited, with undo). The module says so through two reads about an entity (D97): `talks.person`, a section on the opened person and, where an administrator chose it, a lens, a column ("Nästa samtal") and marks on Personal, and `talks.unit`, a section on the unit page. One person's talk is a drawer over the page its "Öppna samtalet" is on, Personal's row menu or the talks section on the person's page, at that page's address with `talk=<personId>` added; closing it leaves the page as it was, and a cold `/staff?talk=<personId>` is Personal with the talk over it (D111 rule 5). The planner takes its deadline and team from the round and the assignments. A manager adds action points for either owner; each puts a card without text in its owner's stream with a `due` timer for the morning after the date; the owner reads and ticks them off under Mina åtgärdspunkter on Hem, with Ångra; open ones carry over to the next round. The text is sealed at rest and is in no audit row, card or model context. The figure "Utvecklingssamtal hållna" belongs to the domain's area. The profile reads `<id>.talks` and `<id>.commitments` are in the protocol; the pages read the composites `clew.talks` and `clew.commitments`; one system answers per installation: the bundled `talks` connector while enabled, else the single connector declaring the profile, shown read-only (`connectors/appraisals` is the fixture, registered as a draft). `scripts/talks-export.ts` and `scripts/talks-erase.ts` export the domain and erase one person.

**Limits and pending work.** No real data until checklist questions 6 and 11 are answered (D96). Not built: the record (preparation, notes, approval), templates, an HR view, a talk without a round, a page for opening a round, an employee adding a point, a manager ticking off an employee's point, booking one person's talk (the calendar has no action about a person). A system behind a connector is told the caller's directory user name in the signed actor (D97); whether that stays the person's key is open. Switching the built-in connector off hides its cards until it is on again. The planner's round read shares a four-second budget with a calendar read, and a person with the manager role who manages no unit causes a logged error on each read. The browser tells "no talks system" from a failure by the refusal's sentence. The section on Hem is not reserved while it loads. `--if-none` on the round script checks and opens in two transactions. No signed-in pass on the development stack and no screen-reader pass yet.

## Kompetens (competence)

**Works in this checkout.** Proposed in D108 and built against the freeze on new surface (D101, the first real integration); it was brought into `main` on 2026-11-11 without the product owner's word on the freeze, which is an open question, and it is switched off until an administrator enables it. A second owned domain, on synthetic data: `modules/competence` with its own tables (migration 0049). One record, a holding, for a competence and a certificate, typed against a catalogue an administrator with `admin.competence` keeps on Administration › Kompetens (add, change, archive; a new validity says what it does to the holdings before it is saved). The manager a person reports to (D99) registers a certificate in a dialog over Personal at `/staff?register=<personId>`. Where a holding stands (valid, expiring within 90 days, expired) is computed from the dates when read, never stored. The bundled `competence` connector has `competence.attention` and `competence.holdings` with declared views, `competence.person`, a read about a person (D97) whose section's page is the registration form, an area with a figure on Hem, and a scan that gives a person a card per holding that has run out or soon will and a manager one card that counts and names nobody. Its row is created disabled: an administrator switches it on under Anslutna system, and until then no area, read, card or route answers. `scripts/competence-export.ts` and `scripts/competence-erase.ts` export the domain and erase one person; `scripts/competence-seed.ts` seeds the development organisation.

**Limits and pending work.** No real data until checklist questions 6 and 11 are answered (D108). Not built: positions over SCIM, requirements, the status `missing`, coverage, share targets and the matrix; the `holdings` profile, the copy from sources and the type mapping; a person's own registration and its approval; `competence.search`; merging two types and bringing an archived one back; revoking a holding from the product (the route exists); the erasure timer after deactivation (an operator runs the script); the read log. The manager's card is refreshed while open and comes again after it is handled for as long as something is due. Nobody has offered `competence.person` as a lens or a column on the development stack. Not seen signed in, the Kompetens section on an opened person has no browser spec, and no screen-reader pass.

## Sources about an entity

**Works in this checkout.** A capability may say its rows are `about` a person or a unit (D97); the loader holds a read to `{ keys }`, one row per key carrying `key`, a table or list view and `model: false`, and an action to the key as its only required input. The core `entities` module checks who may be asked about (for a person: the reader, the people on units they manage and other active people of their organisation; for a unit: one they manage or belong to), sends keys in calls of at most 200 under a four-second budget per source with cancellation, checks authority again after the batch and stores nothing (`GET /api/entities/:kind`, `/rows`, `/:key`, `/:key/section`). An administrator offers a read as a lens and chooses at most three columns per kind on the connector's page, audited as `connector.lens` and `connector.column`. Sources: `talks.person`, `talks.unit`, `hr.person`, `appraisals.person`, `qlik.unit`. The signed actor carries the caller's `userName`. `connector-check` probes the batch, an unknown key and keys out of scope, with `--keys` and `--as` for real people.

**Limits and pending work.** Entity kinds are person and unit only: no source for Kompetenser, Introduktion, access cards or cases, and no rooms. The person's key on the wire is the user name, provisionally. Never a model tool; no two-source list as an assistant answer. No page lists units, so a unit read is a section only. The column limit is counted without a lock. The conformance check warns above 1.5 seconds while the module allows four.

## The opened person and the unit page

**Works in this checkout.** A person is a page (`/people/<key>`, D111 rule 3), reached from a name on Personal or any row that names them; nothing draws a person beside a list, and `/staff?person=<key>` redirects to the page with the list as `from`, `person-section` as `section` and an open card kept. Since D112 item 5 (board Person) the page has the work item's page shape (`work-item-detail`). The header holds the way back (the page in `from` when Clew can name it, else Personal for the reader's own people), their unit on the same line, the avatar, the name and their title, and every action about them with "Fråga Clew": two buttons, the rest under "Fler" grouped by system, "Åtgärder" the width of the column on a phone; the source's refusal stands under the buttons. The main column holds "Behöver dig" (the reader's work items about them, drawn as the work item's own row and opening beside the page) and then every source that has something about them, one under the other with a rule between them, each with its heading, its source's name as a badge (Clew for Clew's own), its row, its fuller read and its own way in ("Öppna samtalet"), each read on its own. There is no tab strip. `?section=<capability>` (or `needs-you`) still lands where it did: the section is scrolled into view and its heading takes the focus, unless something opened beside or over the page holds it. The details rail (340 px, "Detaljer") holds what the directory says that the page has (title, user name, "Chef: Du" for the reader's own people) under "Katalogen", and "Tillhör" with their unit, a link for a unit the reader may open; at 1024 px and below, and while the panel holds something beside the page, it is behind "Visa detaljer" in a fullscreen dialog. A source that does not answer says so in its section with "Försök igen". `/units/<id>` is the unit as a page in the same shape, reached from Läget on Hem and from any row that names the unit: its actions and "Fråga Clew" in the header, "Behöver dig" and a section per source (its row, its fuller read as a short list, its way in such as "Visa i Personal") in the main column, and in the rail its facts: a unit, the reader's role on it, for a unit the reader manages Clew's own count of its people with "Öppna Personal", and "Enheter under", the units under it whose managers report to the reader, each a link to its page ([D117](../../decisions/D117-a-manager-follows-up-the-units-below-and-a-source-is-told-them.md)). A manager opens those units too (relation `below`, "Enhetens chef rapporterar till dig"), and a read about a unit and a view opened on the page carry the reader's followed units to the connector as `units` in the signed actor. Dialogstöd at `/units` lists the units the reader follows up side by side, "Mina enheter" and "Enheter under dig", one column per source about a unit with its row in short (the heading naming the measure and the source; no column where no unit of the group has a row), each name opening the unit; "Att ta upp" on top counts the units per state that needs action or watching, the units with the most of it stand first, and on a phone each unit is a card. A unit's page has its key figures as a row of cards above its sections, and a trail back to Dialogstöd with a menu of the other units. The follow-up dialogue ([D118](../../decisions/D118-the-follow-up-dialogue-is-an-owned-domain.md)) is Clew's own connector `dialogue`: its section "Uppföljningsdialog" stands first on a unit's page with "Öppna dialogen", Dialogstöd has a column for it, and "Att ta upp" names the area and the units ("Uppföljningsdialog · Åtgärd krävs: Verksamhet – Bergsåkers skola"), each unit a link, into the drawer for the dialogue's lines and to the unit's page for the rest. The drawer over the unit's page (`?dialog=<unitId>`, a sheet on a phone) has the configured areas as tabs, each with its state, and opens on the area that needs action: where it stands (a measured area's figure from its source, an assessed area's state per dimension with who set it and the history), what to talk about, then a new assessment and the activities, the forms behind "Ny bedömning" and "Lägg till aktivitet"; activities are added, changed, marked done with a note, opened again and, once done, removed behind a confirmation. An activity goes to the unit's manager as a card in Att göra about the unit, with a timer the morning after a due date when it has one. Areas and questions are loaded by an operator (`scripts/dialogue-areas.ts`; the development stack loads made-up ones); nothing is migrated from big-boss-board, and no screen-reader pass has been made. The person's page and the unit's page hold the panel beside their column (D111): a work item under Behöver dig and the one an action makes open there, with the address kept, and closing leaves the page as it was and gives the focus back to what opened it; where the panel does not fit it is the drawer over the page. A person or unit outside the rule is the page's own "finns inte". A work item's "Granska N" beside the person's or the unit's page opens its review over that page, and the talks section's "Öppna samtalet" opens the person's talk over it; closing either leaves the page with its section, its way back and the item beside it (D111 rule 5).

**Limits and pending work.** No rail entry leads to a person or a unit. The board's facts that have no data behind them are not drawn: the person's manager by name, phone, e-mail, workplace and start date in "Detaljer" (a source's section, such as a directory's contact read, carries those where one exists), the unit's path above the unit, and on a unit's page the unit above and the managers by name (the units below in the reporting line are listed since D117, one level, never the whole branch). The browser scenarios run on routed data, including sources the server does not have (Kompetenser) and an action and a work item about a unit; they were rewritten for D112 item 5 and passed on 2026-11-12. No signed-in pass on the development stack and no screen-reader pass.

## Subjects on cards and their erasure

**Works in this checkout.** A card row, a search hit and a table, list or detail record may name a person or a unit (`subject`); the name is then a link to them, with the page as the way back. Clew's own action point cards carry the employee as a card subject that is never drawn or read by the model. Migration 0045 adds the index from entity to cards, written with the card's rows on both adapters; it gives "Behöver dig" and the counts under "Just nu". Search hits with the same subject merge into one. `scripts/subject-erase.ts` erases one person or unit from every card, audited with counts only.

**Limits and pending work.** The calendar's planning card names nobody, so it is not indexed. No source sets `subject` on a search hit, so a merged hit has been seen in tests only. Cards stored before 0045 are indexed when their rows are next written. Erasure leaves the rows' text, and a source that still names the entity names it again on its next scan. A person's key in the card tables is weighed against principle 7 in D97's open questions.

## Actions about a person

**Works in this checkout.** `POST /api/entities/:kind/:key/actions` starts an action from the entity's menu without the model: the key goes into its `param`, a propose runs as the person and its draft is the card, an execute becomes a card whose button is the human decision. The card, its first message and the `thread.started` audit row share a transaction; the same request while the card is open gives the same card. The web opens the card beside the page the action was started on (D111 rule 4): beside Personal's list, beside the person's or the unit's page, where every action about the entity stands in the page's header (D112 item 5), and as the drawer over the page where the panel does not fit; closing it gives the focus back to the control that started it, on Personal the row's menu button. The HR fixture's `hr.prepareAbsence` ("Registrera frånvaro") is the one source.

**Limits and pending work.** No calendar action about a person ("Boka möte"): the calendar has no propose capability. No source offers an action about a unit; the unit's page draws one when a source does, seen on routed data only. A follower or an execute with `review` cannot be started from a key.
