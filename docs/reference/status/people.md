One page of [implementation status](../status.md). Each entry says what works in this checkout, what limits it or is still to do, and where the code and tests that show it are.

## Personal

**Works in this checkout.** A manager's place in the rail, after Kalender (D94, D112 item 1), read from the entities API (D97) and knowing no feature: the reader's reports (D99, the organisation tree), one card per unit they manage: the people assigned to the unit and, down the organisation tree, the managers of the nearest units that have one and the people on a unit nobody leads; never the people those managers lead. The talks round and the calendar's team read the same list. "Alla" shows the columns an administrator chose, the source named under each heading, and "Just nu" with the marks of the offered lenses and the reader's cards about the person ("1 att agera på", "N förslag"): one card opens beside the list (`?card=`), several lead to the person's page at Behöver dig (`/people/<key>?section=needs-you&from=…`; an old `?cards=` address leads there), D111 amended. "Visa" chooses a lens, kept in the address as `?show=`, and the list becomes that source's view sorted by need. Each row has a menu of the actions about a person, grouped by system; an action's card opens beside the list, with the lens kept, and closing it gives the focus back to the row's menu button. A name leads to the person's page, the one way a person is shown (D111 rule 3); an address that drew a person beside the list (`?person=`) leads there. The panel stands beside the list from 1336 px, as on every page, and the list gives way: where the table no longer keeps its least width beside the panel (Astryx's `resolveColumnWidths`; 1020 px for the development stack's two columns, so below a 1780 px window) it is drawn as its rows; below 1336 px the panel is the drawer over it. Personal never gives way to Hem for a card. A source that does not answer is said once with "Försök igen"; the rest stands. Someone who manages nobody gets the page's empty state.

**Limits and pending work.** The hard-wired talk columns are gone; the talk is reached from the talks section's "Öppna samtalet", and its drawer still reads `GET /api/staff`, which answers 403 to anyone who manages no unit. No notes. A person who reports from a unit below is listed like the people assigned to the unit: the page does not say which unit they lead, which the earlier role column did ("Chef för …"), and the browser scenario for it left with that column. Managers of the same unit are listed with each other. With "Alla"'s column weights the table needs 1020 px, so on most laptop windows a card is the drawer over Personal rather than beside it. "1 att agera på" beside "1 förslag" leads to a list of one card: the list's read names a card only when the person has exactly one in all (`attention.threadId`). Only the development stack's synthetic directory has filled it, and the page has not been looked at signed in as a manager with a report from below. No signed-in pass on the development stack with the new list and no screen-reader pass.

## Utvecklingssamtal (talks)

**Works in this checkout.** Clew's first owned domain (D96), on synthetic data: `modules/talks` with its own tables (migration 0043). An operator opens a round with `scripts/talk-round.ts`. Per person on a manager's units the talk is missing, booked (read from the calendar) or held (the manager marks it, audited, with undo). The module says so through two reads about an entity (D97): `talks.person`, a section on the opened person and, where an administrator chose it, a lens, a column ("Nästa samtal") and marks on Personal, and `talks.unit`, a section on the unit page. One person's talk is a drawer over the page its "Öppna samtalet" is on, Personal's row menu or the talks section on the person's page, at that page's address with `talk=<personId>` added; closing it leaves the page as it was, and a cold `/staff?talk=<personId>` is Personal with the talk over it (D111 rule 5). The planner takes its deadline and team from the round and the assignments. A manager adds action points for either owner; each puts a card without text in its owner's stream with a `due` timer for the morning after the date; the owner reads and ticks them off under Mina åtgärdspunkter on Hem, with Ångra; open ones carry over to the next round. The text is sealed at rest and is in no audit row, card or model context. The figure "Utvecklingssamtal hållna" belongs to the domain's area. The profile reads `<id>.talks` and `<id>.commitments` are in the protocol; the pages read the composites `clew.talks` and `clew.commitments`; one system answers per installation: the bundled `talks` connector while enabled, else the single connector declaring the profile, shown read-only (`connectors/appraisals` is the fixture, registered as a draft). `scripts/talks-export.ts` and `scripts/talks-erase.ts` export the domain and erase one person.

**Limits and pending work.** No real data until checklist questions 6 and 11 are answered (D96). Not built: the record (preparation, notes, approval), templates, an HR view, a talk without a round, a page for opening a round, an employee adding a point, a manager ticking off an employee's point, booking one person's talk (the calendar has no action about a person). A system behind a connector is told the caller's directory user name in the signed actor (D97); whether that stays the person's key is open. Switching the built-in connector off hides its cards until it is on again. The planner's round read shares a four-second budget with a calendar read, and a person with the manager role who manages no unit causes a logged error on each read. The browser tells "no talks system" from a failure by the refusal's sentence. The section on Hem is not reserved while it loads. `--if-none` on the round script checks and opens in two transactions. No signed-in pass on the development stack and no screen-reader pass yet.

## Kompetens (competence)

**Works in this checkout.** Proposed in D108 and built against the freeze on new surface (D101, the first real integration); it was brought into `main` on 2026-11-11 without the product owner's word on the freeze, which the product owner lifted on 2026-11-12, so it stays; it is switched off until an administrator enables it. A second owned domain, on synthetic data: `modules/competence` with its own tables (migration 0049). One record, a holding, for a competence and a certificate, typed against a catalogue an administrator with `admin.competence` keeps on Administration › Kompetens (add, change, archive; a new validity says what it does to the holdings before it is saved), whose row the rail draws only while the module is switched on and the person holds `admin.competence` (2026-11-12); an address from before while it is off gets the page's own answer. The manager a person reports to (D99) registers a certificate in a dialog over the page they are on: Personal at `/staff?register=<personId>`, or, since 2026-11-13, the person's own page (`/people/<key>?register=<personId>`), since the section's way in is a query alone (`?register={id}`); closing it replaces its address, so Back never opens it again. Where a holding stands (valid, expiring within 90 days, expired) is computed from the dates when read, never stored. The bundled `competence` connector has `competence.attention` and `competence.holdings` with declared views, `competence.person`, a read about a person (D97) whose section's page is the registration form, an area with a figure on Hem, and a scan that gives a person a card per holding that has run out or soon will and a manager one card that counts and names nobody. Its row is created disabled: an administrator switches it on under Anslutna system, and until then no area, read, card or route answers. `scripts/competence-export.ts` and `scripts/competence-erase.ts` export the domain (`clew.competence/2` since 2026-11-12) and erase one person; `scripts/competence-seed.ts` seeds the development organisation.

What a source holds is copied in (D108 item 9, 2026-11-12, migration 0055). Every system that answers the holdings profile is read once a day at 04:00 and when an administrator presses "Läs in nu", by the durable workflow `competenceCopy`, never two runs of one source at once; each read is one transaction with one audit row of counts. A key is matched to an active person by user name, a key that matches nobody is counted and never stored, and a repeated read changes only when the rows were read. A row is revoked for absence only after a complete read of a source that says it reports withdrawals. A page the source does not answer is asked again twice, a refused cursor starts the sweep again once, and any other failure ends the read, which keeps what it read and is recorded with the failure's kind. A copied row whose type an administrator has mapped counts in the status, the reads, the figure and the cards exactly as a registered one; one whose type is not mapped is listed on a person's holdings under the source's label as "räknas inte" and counts towards nothing. Every listed copied row shows its source and when it was read. Administration › Kompetens › Källor (`/admin/competence/sources`) shows each source's last read and how it went, starts a read, and maps each of its types to the catalogue in a dialog with an address, re-typing what is already copied. Removing a connector removes what it put in the register; `scripts/competence-forget-source.ts` does it for one that stays, `scripts/competence-read.ts` starts a read and prints its counts, and `scripts/competence-map.ts` maps a type (with `--development`, the development learning platform's seeded courses).

Requirements, coverage and share targets (D108 items 4 to 6, 2026-11-12, migrations 0056 and 0057). A person's positions arrive over SCIM as `position:` groups (see [identity](identity.md)). A requirement names a type and one of a position, a unit or a person; an administrator with `admin.competence` sets the first two on Administration › Kompetens › Krav (`/admin/competence/requirements`), where share targets are kept too, and the manager a person reports to sets one for that person alone (a route; no page yet), which the administrator never sees ([D118](../../decisions/D118-an-administrators-capability-is-no-relation-to-a-person.md)). A required type a person has no counting holding of is `missing` ("Saknas"), computed when read like the other statuses, and it counts with what has run out in the person's section, on Personal, in the figure, in `competence.attention` and in the cards; a person's words are, in order, Utgånget, Saknas, Går ut snart, Giltigt, Inget registrerat. `competence.unit` is a read about a unit for the manager who leads it: coverage per required type and each share target's share, for the reader's own reports only, however many others the branch holds, since a row that mixed the two could be subtracted from another to single a person out. People the reader does not manage are counted only in a share target, which is measured once, over what it was set on: a unit's on that unit's read for the manager who leads it, an organisation-wide one on the Krav page as a figure that names nobody. A target is shown when the people in it whom the reader does not manage are none or at least five, and withheld ("För få för att visas") otherwise, as are two targets of one type whose positions overlap. A requirement and a share target name a position by its code and a unit by its id (migration 0057), so they outlive a position group that is removed and pushed again and can name a unit nobody is placed on; one whose position or unit is gone is listed as "Finns inte längre" and counts towards nothing. A card closes when its cause is gone and comes back when the cause does: the scan reports a complete observation, a person's card follows the person and the type (not a holding's row, so a source read again does not re-issue it), and its identity changes with the end date, the requirements that apply and the last revocation; the manager's card is one occurrence that comes back when its counts change, up or down. A position that disappears and returns with the same code brings the same identity, so that card does not reopen. Cards stored by the scan as it was before are retired once by an operator with `scripts/competence-cards-reset.ts`.

A person's own registration (D108 item 7, 2026-11-13, migration 0059). "Lägg till" on `/competence` (`?add=new`) registers a certificate for the reader. It is stored as waiting, counts towards nothing, and opens a work item for each manager the person reports to, in the same transaction; the card's text names nobody and no certificate. A manager the person reports to now approves or rejects it on the matrix (`/competence/matrix?approve=<holdingId>`, from the card, or from "Granska" in a cell), which closes every card in the same transaction; an approved registration keeps the evidence `self` with who decided and when, and counts from then. Skills with levels (D120 items 1 to 6, 2026-11-13, migration 0060). `skill` ("Färdighet") is a kind in the catalogue; a holding of such a type carries a level, Grundläggande, Självständig or Expert, and no end date, one per person and type. A person rates their own on `/competence` with the mark "Vill utveckla" if they wish; it is listed at once as "Egen bedömning" to them and to a manager they report to, and opens no work item. A manager confirms the level, or sets another, from the matrix (`?validate=<holdingId>`), or registers a skill for a report; a new rating by the person makes it their own again. A requirement on a skill names a least level, and only a confirmed skill at or above it meets the requirement; otherwise the type is missing and the level held is shown beside it. A source type cannot be mapped to a skill. The export is `clew.competence/6`. Since the review fixes of 2026-11-13 (migration 0060, `rated_at`; export `clew.competence/7`): a person holds a skill once (a second add is refused, and a change is its own action, `PUT /own/skills/:id`); removing one's own skill revokes the row and is audited as `competence.skill_removed`; a manager cannot revoke a skill the person rated themselves; a type mapped to a source cannot be made into or out of a skill; and a person's "saknas" card for a skill is a new card when the level held or its confirmation changes. Card text names no level.

The catalogue at scale (D120 item 7, D108 item 2, 2026-11-13, migration 0062; export `clew.competence/8`). Administration › Kompetens › Importera (`/admin/competence/import`, `admin.competence`) reads a CSV in the browser with the parser the server runs again on every row (`packages/contracts/src/competence-import.ts`): Clew's own columns (`name, area, kind, validityMonths, uri`) or ESCO's skills file, recognised by its header, whose released rows become types of kind `skill` under an area the administrator chooses. Rows already in the catalogue (by address, else by name) are marked and skipped, so an import can be run again; the page lists rows 50 at a time under a search, sends a large choice in parts of 1 000, and prints ESCO's acknowledgement for an ESCO file. Limits: 30 000 rows a file, 20 000 types an organisation. A type keeps the address it came with (`source_uri`). On the catalogue, which is now searched and paged, a type is merged into another ("Slå ihop med …": holdings, requirements, share targets and source mappings move in one transaction, the numbers shown first; where a person holds a skill of both, the confirmed one, then the higher level, is kept and the other revoked; refused between a skill and another kind, into an archived or merged type, and while a person has a registration waiting for both) and an archived type is brought back ("Återställ"); a merge is not undone. Audited as `competence.types_imported`, `competence.type_merged` and `competence.type_restored`. Cards of the merged type close at the next refresh and are opened under the target; a manager's count card is issued once more. No real ESCO download has been read: the importer is written against the published column names, and the file's knowledge concepts would be imported as skills too. Since the review fixes of 2026-11-13: a merge locks the holdings of both types before it plans (the lock order is written on `CompetenceTx`), so a confirmation or a level change made at the same moment is neither lost nor a deadlock; the numbers shown before a merge leave out requirements set on one person, waiting and rejected registrations and deactivated people, and say "färre än 5" below five; names are normalised (NFC, no invisible characters) so look-alikes are one type, and an address is `http(s)` or `urn`; the catalogue's search and page are in the address; a part of a large import that the server refuses says which rows were not saved. Left: a source never read before has no row to lock, so its first read racing a merge could copy rows onto the merged type until its next read.

Erasure after a person leaves (D108 item 14, 2026-11-13, migrations 0065 and 0066). An operator sets a retention period per organisation with `scripts/competence-retention.ts --organisation <id> --days <n>` (1 to 3650; `--off` clears it; no argument prints the period and how many wait, are overdue or fall due within a day); there is no default, and while none is set nothing is erased. A deactivation over SCIM (PATCH, PUT, DELETE, or a person created inactive) tells competence through the people module's `leavers` port inside the provisioning transaction, which records an intent (`competence_erasure`: the person and since when); a failure there is logged and never fails or delays the deactivation by more than two seconds. No workflow sleeps per person: a sweep that runs at boot and every hour beside the copy's guard records deactivations no intent records, cancels intents of people who are active again, and erases what is due, one person and one transaction at a time, so a deployment or a process death strands nothing. An erasure is due the period's days after the deactivation and never sooner than a day after the period was set or changed. It erases what the operator's script erases (holdings and copied rows, own registrations, requirements set on the person, every competence card in the person's own stream), after re-checking under the intent's lock that the person is still inactive and a period is set; one audit row `competence.person_erased` with the cause `retention` and counts. A reactivation cancels the intent and nothing registered in Clew was touched. A round that leaves erasures due or finds one more than a day overdue logs a warning. The operator's `competence-erase.ts` goes through the same logic (cause `operator`). Limits: with no instance running nothing is erased until the next start; where the port failed the period counts from when the sweep found the person; what the people module holds (the person, positions, memberships) stays, the read log keeps rows with the id, and the audit rows written earlier keep what they recorded (the type, dates and a skill's level beside the person's id), so the audit log still tells what the erased rows told (an open question); a directory that deactivates people on long leave would have their manager-registered holdings erased (an open question); the operator's export still includes deactivated people until they are erased; talks does not use the port.

What needs attention, one definition, and the faces of the register made to agree (the product owner's definition and the review of 2026-11-13). ## Actions about a person

**Works in this checkout.** `POST /api/entities/:kind/:key/actions` starts an action from the entity's menu without the model: the key goes into its `param`, a propose runs as the person and its draft is the card, an execute becomes a card whose button is the human decision. The card, its first message and the `thread.started` audit row share a transaction; the same request while the card is open gives the same card. The web opens the card beside the page the action was started on (D111 rule 4): beside Personal's list, beside the person's or the unit's page, where every action about the entity stands in the page's header (D112 item 5), and as the drawer over the page where the panel does not fit; closing it gives the focus back to the control that started it, on Personal the row's menu button. The HR fixture's `hr.prepareAbsence` ("Registrera frånvaro") is the one source.

## The assistant asked about a person

**Works in this checkout.** [D115](../../decisions/D115-the-assistant-resolves-a-person-the-asker-names-and-hands-the-model-the-work-about-them-not-health-rows.md). A question that names one of the people the asker manages ("Anna har varit sjuk i två veckor, vad gör jag?") is resolved by Clew among them alone: a whole name in any case, or a name written with a capital, by full name, first name or family name. With a model, the prompt says who the name is and lists the asker's open work about them (cards whose rows name them) by title, source, last day and buttons; the model may look a name up itself with `clew.findPerson`, only among people the asker's own words in the conversation could name, and reads the routine through the HR fixture's `hr.rehabRoutine`. A card about anyone the conversation names is handed by its title, last day and buttons alone, chipped or not, in that turn and the later ones; a read about the person never reaches the model. The work items are drawn in the turn; with one person and one item, the model may draft its action and "Granska och …" opens the action's dialog. Without a model, the router answers with the work item under Behöver dig and draws it, asks which when the name is several people, and otherwise goes on as before; its figure rule answers only questions about the figures, never one that asks what to do or names someone. Several matches are named; nobody is said to be nobody. The turn's audit row lists the tools and the cards, never a name.

**Limits and pending work.** No model has run it outside the scripted tests: the development stack has no model configured, so the router answers there, and Clew learns of `hr.rehabRoutine` only once the HR connector's manifest is fetched again on Anslutna system. A work item's title is handed as the source wrote it, and `hr.whoIsOn` still hands a manager's model the names of the absent with the kind of absence (both in the open questions). A pronoun in a later turn does not carry the person over. People on other units are not resolved. A model marked as safe for health data is the intended next step and is not built. The interface's status line that a conversation about a person tells the model nothing still holds for "Fråga Clew" beside a person's page, which sends no chip: the model learns who it is only when the asker writes the name.
