# Implementation status: calendar

One page of [implementation status](../status.md). Each entry says what works in this checkout, what limits it or is still to do, and where the code and tests that show it are.

## Calendar

**Works in this checkout.** CalDAV adapter and core calendar paths. The Microsoft 365 adapter passes the same contract against a local fake of Microsoft Graph, which runs in-process in the unit suite and as the `graph` service of the base development stack, self-seeded, never imported by the server (D69, amended). The development directory is a mixed estate: Mikael's calendar is Microsoft 365 over the fake, Hillevi's and Hassan's are CalDAV; `CALENDAR_ADAPTER` binds everyone to one. The calendar's reads declare their output schemas and Swedish labels, and `calendar.rooms` is a declared table view that an administrator can put in the menu as Mötesrum. An event's `id` is the source's own handle and may be up to 1 000 characters: a Nextcloud handle is the base64 of its CalDAV path, over a hundred, and until 2026-11-11 the registry refused every `calendar.today`, `calendar.week` and `calendar.day` answer with an event in it for a person on Nextcloud, which left the model's calendar tools and the action dialog's day without data.

**Limits and pending work.** The Microsoft 365 adapter has not met a real tenant, and no delegated token reaches it, so it runs only in development. Today, the week and a day are drawn by the calendar family, not by a declared view (D32). No signed-in browser pass on the Microsoft 365 binding yet.

## The calendar page

**Works in this checkout.** [D112](../../decisions/D112-the-interface-is-rebuilt-on-astryx-templates-a-queue-one-detail-shape-one-panel.md) item 9, board Calendar. Kalender keeps its own page ([D126](../../decisions/D126-one-page-per-thing-one-astryx-template-per-page-type.md), D27). Since the pass of 2026-11-18 (canvas "Clew · Kalender, a pass") the grid is the sheet's own content, without a frame of its own: the week is seven days and the day twenty-four hours, 07–18 fills what is seen at any window height and the hours before and after are a scroll away inside the grid; the days and hours outside the working week are drawn as the ground, a meeting has a muted fill, and the entry in the pane is told by the accent. The steps stand beside the title. An event or a proposal chosen in the grid opens in the page's own pane in the end slot (`entry` in the address), the drawer over the grid below 1340 px, on a short screen and on a phone, with the header of the pane beside a conversation: a meeting with when it is under its name and, under its buttons, where, who organises it and who comes, "Du är inbjuden" when the person does not organise it, and "Flytta …" and "Ställ in …" on a booking of their own; a proposal with "Förslag, inte bokat", the round's last day, when and with whom, and its existing decisions: "Skicka inbjudan" for that time, "Flytta …", "Föreslå andra tider", "Ångra" while the calendar allows it, and its name in the pane's header is the link to its work item's page. "Ny bokning" (the button, a click on an hour, a span dragged) and "Flytta …" are forms in that same pane: the time is a dashed block on the grid that is dragged and stretched, the fields (Dag, Börjar, Slutar) say the same time and are the keyboard path, the buttons stay at the pane's foot, and a booking's availability is read by itself as the time, the guests or the room change. A booking being made is kept while another entry stands in the pane. On a phone the two forms are the dialogs they were; "Ställ in …" asks in a dialog at every width. "Förslag N" in the header lists the proposals that wait, each a link to its week with it open in the pane; An entry and the range are asked about in place ([D140](../../decisions/D140-the-assistant-is-asked-in-place-where-its-answer-is-drawn-on-the-page.md), stage 1): an entry in the pane has the assistant's box at the pane's foot, and the range is asked about with the corner's "Fråga AI", which opens the assistant's pane beside the page ([D141](../../decisions/D141-the-assistant-is-asked-beside-every-page-through-one-button-and-one-pane-of-the-page-frame.md)); the conversation is drawn by the same parts as on the conversation's page; the pane draws the whole conversation about the entry with "Öppna samtalet" and finds it again when the entry is reopened, and an answer that proposes a new time for the person's own meeting (a prepared decision, `calendar.move`) fills in "Flytta …" and draws its block on the grid; the person saves it. Beside a conversation the same answer draws a row with the time and "Visa i kalendern". When the calendar does not answer, the page's head says "Kalendern svarar inte" (since when, when known) with "Försök igen", the grid keeps the weeks it has read, and with nothing read it says the week cannot be shown rather than drawing an empty one; it is spoken once in the session. A person with no calendar (not switched on for them, or no account bound) gets the page's "Ingen kalender är kopplad till dig" with who connects one, and the pages' row draws Kalender only while the calendar is the person's to see ([D116, amended 2026-11-12](../../decisions/D116-a-panel-is-a-peek-at-one-thing-a-list-is-a-page-and-every-view-has-an-address.md)).

**Limits and pending work.** A meeting someone else organises has no buttons: the calendar source offers the invitee no reply of their own, so "Ändra mitt svar …" (the board) is not drawn, and the person's own answer is not shown. An answer can propose a new time for a meeting of the person's own and nothing else yet: finding a time for several people and a prepared booking are stage 2 of D140 and not built, so a question about the range is answered as text, and a proposal is not moved by asking. On the development stack the assistant filled in a time it was given; asked to choose one it wrongly answered that none was free. Where the pane holds a conversation its body does not scroll as one, so the facts over it cannot be scrolled in a very low window (1.4.10 not checked by hand). An answer with sources shows their numbers without the list. On a phone the head keeps its two full rows and has no "Fråga om veckan"; an entry opened there is the drawer over the grid. Below 1340 px the pane is a modal drawer, so the block of a booking or a move cannot be dragged there; the fields are the way. The grid's own scroller has no tab stop (one would break the table roles FullCalendar gives the grid): every entry is a tab stop that comes into view with the focus and every hour is reached through the forms' fields, but an empty stretch outside the office day cannot be scrolled to from a keyboard. FullCalendar's Standard edition has no horizontal scroll of days (`dayMinWidth` is Premium), so seven days share the width, and no scrolling from one week to the next. The browser scenarios (`tests/a11y/calendar.spec.ts`) passed on routed data on 2026-11-18, and the pass was looked at signed in on the development stack that day; no screen-reader pass.

## Calendar follow-up

**Works in this checkout.** Approved bounded policies, two reminders maximum per invitation, two working days apart, 30-day expiry, current assignment checks and transactional invitee cards/counters/audit. An invitee is found by the person's id, which a proposal stores beside the address it was drawn with (migration 0056), so a changed work address keeps the reminders, the booking and the round's count. Confirmation and dispatch each check that only the invitee holds the drawn address in the manager's current team. If that check fails, the manager can draw new times with the current address. Replies to a sent invitation are matched on the address it used.

**Limits and pending work.** Legacy invitations are paused for review; no implicit authorization for new automatic policy.
