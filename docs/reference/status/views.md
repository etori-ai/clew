# Implementation status: views and documents

One page of [implementation status](../status.md). Each entry says what works in this checkout, what limits it or is still to do, and where the code and tests that show it are.

## Views and menu tools

**Works in this checkout.** `table`, `list`, `detail`, `text`, `document`, `record`, `metrics`, `series`, `activity`; controls; same-connector row opens; summary-only model reads; administrator-selected tool pages; an optional `unit` on a number field and `noun` on a table or list (D71, amended); a record's `subject`, the `tones` key `*`, and `beside` and `compact` on a field (D97). Where a view opens is decided by its kind ([D116](../../decisions/D116-a-panel-is-a-peek-at-one-thing-a-list-is-a-page-and-every-view-has-an-address.md), 2026-11-12): a `table` or a `list`, the search's hits and an undeclared answer with rows are a page, `/tools/<capability>?input=…&title=…&from=…`, for every read the person may call, placed in the rail or not, with the way back named at its top and Back returning to the opener with the focus on it; any other kind opens in the panel beside the page or the conversation it was opened from, and a `record` or a `document` there links to its page by its name in the panel's header (2026-11-13: the button "Öppna som sida" was removed; the title is the way in). `GET /api/areas` lists the reads the person may open with their kinds (`views`); a read the person may not call and an unknown one are the same "Verktyget finns inte". A person can ask about a view's page from the page ([D50](../../decisions/D50-the-conversation-is-the-column-and-the-panel-holds-what-it-is-about.md)). A tool's page is a table with a filter bar and an inspector ([D112](../../decisions/D112-the-interface-is-rebuilt-on-astryx-templates-a-queue-one-detail-shape-one-panel.md) item 6, board Tool): the title with the system's badge, then the view's search field and choice with the count of its rows; a row's own read (`view.open`) opens in the page's panel as the inspector, with the box under it, the address holding the selection, the row marked as the current one (`aria-current`, and a dot named "Visas bredvid" in the table), and ✕ or Esc giving the focus back to the row's name. A row that names a person or a unit (`subject`) leads to that page by its name, and only there (2026-11-13: a person's name always opens the person); such a row's own read (`view.open`, or a combined list's row `view`) is a link of its own, named with the read's title for the row ("Mönster för Anna Lindqvist"), at the row's end in a list and in a column headed with the read's title in a table. A view that names no entity keeps its title as the way to the row's read. A read of Clew's own may declare the page its rows are worked on (`Capability.page`, listed as `ViewEntry.page`; a remote connector cannot): whatever opens it without input leads there, its own page staying for an address that names it. A read of Clew's own may say how many rows it left out (`omitted` beside the rows): the count is of them all and the rows end with "Visar de första N. M till visas inte.". "Inget av det sparas på Startsidan" stands under a view only when its rows live in a system outside Clew (`external`: the connector's kind is not Clew's own domain, D3, so every connector over the protocol and the calendar); Clew's own registers say nothing of the kind. A status standing alone is written with its first letter capitalised (`statusLabel` over `VALUE_WORDS`, "Utgånget"), and a day is short in a row ("13 okt. 2026") and long among one record's facts or in a sentence ("13 oktober 2026"; `shortDay` and `longDay` in `panel-labels.ts`).

**Limits and pending work.** No connector-rendered app/iframe, series/sections view kinds or general page builder (a figure is an area's, D54, not a view kind). The catalogue is closed by [D100](../../decisions/D100-the-vision-is-narrowed-to-what-clew-can-keep-today.md); a read without a declared view is not drawn, and the default drawn from its output schema that D100 calls for is not built. Under D116 a read without a declared view (the search's hits) cannot be known as a list before it answers: what opens it names it beside the page, and the panel sends it on to its page once its rows arrive, in place in the history. A list or a table is never beside a conversation any more, so a row of one no longer goes into that conversation's box with "Lägg till i samtalet" (rows under an answer still can); a list asked about with "Fråga Clew" continues as a conversation with its chip and without the list beside it. The on-demand screenshots `screens.views.spec.ts` and `screens.pattern.spec.ts` still open lists in the panel and are to be redrawn.

## Document and semantic catalogue

**Works in this checkout.** Shared PDF page/zoom/selectable text and text alternative, isolated DOCX preview, XLSX sheet/value preview and images; original download, attachment navigation, versioned record composition, typed metrics, line/bar charts with equivalent tables and source activity. Authenticated binary delivery verifies current scope, MIME, size, digest and versions. Signed review evidence is checked at new card, generic and job approvals; the finance fixture atomically rejects changed versions.

**Limits and pending work.** Markdown file previews deferred. Office previews are approximate and bounded; no OCR, PDF conversion, ranges or durable reading-state store. Legacy pending finance cards need a new scan after manifest refresh. Real-source validation and manual screen-reader checks remain required.

## Views, answers and small places

**Works in this checkout.** One header for every view kind, drawn by `DeclaredView`: the area's chip (the area that offers the capability, else its menu row), the source and the count in the view's noun, the controls or "Visa allt" at its end; on a tool page the title at level 1 comes first with the system's badge at the end of its line and the filter bar under it, the view's controls and the count (D112 item 6, in the one page header every surface shares, [D74](../../decisions/D74-one-page-header-for-every-surface.md)); a record's title at level 2. A number with a unit reads "12 platser"; a status is one badge whose word is its label for a yes/no, coloured only where the view gives a tone. Under the latest answer the view is a card with three rows and "3 av 7 rader". Search hits lead their meta line with the source's glyph and word, an old review date is a warning badge. Composer tokens, the person's chips and `@` rows carry their system's glyph; the review's page has the mark row and the counter badge. A silent system is no longer a banner at the top of every page (D71 artboard 8, replaced by [D116, amended 2026-11-12](../../decisions/D116-a-panel-is-a-peek-at-one-thing-a-list-is-a-page-and-every-view-has-an-address.md)): it is said where its content is missing, on its work items and in the block or page whose read failed, and spoken once in the session ([Att göra](work.md#att-göra), [Hem under the box](interface.md#hem-under-the-box), [the calendar page](calendar.md#the-calendar-page)).

**Limits and pending work.** A text view's own execute ("Jag har läst" under the text) is not built: a view has no execute and the receipt stays the card's. Astryx's table header and `MetadataList` labels keep Astryx's type. No signed-in pass on the development stack or screen-reader pass yet.

**Evidence.** [D71](../../decisions/D71-three-type-levels-and-an-area-mark-whose-colour-is-the-system.md), [view](../../../apps/web/src/components/view.tsx), [a silent system](../../../apps/web/src/components/silent-sources.tsx), [unit tests](../../../tests/unit/views.test.ts), [labels](../../../tests/unit/panel-labels.test.ts), [browser evidence](../../../tests/a11y/views.spec.ts)

## Läget's figures

**Works in this checkout.** One tile per area whose connector answers a figure on `POST /clew/figure`, after the groups on Hem, in the areas' order (D54): the connector's label, tone, value, comparison, progress and `opens` (a same-connector read whose view needs no input, checked by the registry); a connector that does not answer is the registry's warning figure. Up to four figure calls run together within a shared two-second budget; current person authority and governance are checked after the batch, and remote requests are cancelled on expiry. `figureItems()` draws the tile (chip, tone in Clew's words, display-3 value, comparison or bar, the view it opens as a link, to its page when it is a list or a table and into the panel when it is one thing (D116), or to the page a read of Clew's own declares for its rows; a sentence without a value; the silent tile; skeletons) and the rail's word on Hem (the line it drew in "Sedan i går" went with that digest on 2026-11-13). An administrator switches a figure off per area on Områden ("Visa nyckeltalet", audited). The fixtures answer artboard 3's figures; the fixed `GET /api/figures` and its module are gone.

**Limits and pending work.** The line under an answer is the views' to mount; the panel has no tabs any more (D111), so the figure's word on a tab is gone. HME has no connector. No signed-in pass on the development stack or screen-reader pass yet.
