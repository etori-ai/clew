# Interface spaces and navigation

Clew gives each space a stable job. **A thing has one page, and a click on its name goes there.** A work item, a person, a unit, a plan, a record and a connected system each have one page; a list or a table is a page too. Nothing opens over a page to show a thing, so a person can always predict what a click does: the name leads to the thing, and Back returns to where they were. The space is decided by what the thing is, never by the page the control was on or by the source that supplied it.

This is the current placement reference. [D83](../decisions/D83-interface-spaces-have-stable-navigation-roles.md) set the spaces and [D111](../decisions/D111-where-something-opens-is-decided-by-what-it-is.md) the rules that place things in them; [D116](../decisions/D116-a-panel-is-a-peek-at-one-thing-a-list-is-a-page-and-every-view-has-an-address.md) made every list a page with an address, and [D126](../decisions/D126-one-page-per-thing-one-astryx-template-per-page-type.md) (2026-11-14) made every thing a page and removed the panel that opened over Hem, Personal and the tools, which [D112](../decisions/D112-the-interface-is-rebuilt-on-astryx-templates-a-queue-one-detail-shape-one-panel.md) had drawn. Where they differ, D126 holds. [D130](../decisions/D130-clews-look-and-layout-are-the-startsidan-prototypes-built-with-astryx.md) (2026-11-18) took the rail away: Clew's places are a row under the shared header's band, and the look and layout are the startsidan prototype's. The panel was at once a peek, a work surface and a conversation, and the same work item opened in it from Hem, in a pane from Att göra and as a page elsewhere; one container per kind of thing removes the question. Everything below was read from the code and the browser specs named under [Implementation and verification](#implementation-and-verification). Connector authors have a shorter version in [Where things open](../guides/where-things-open.md).

## Widths

- **The page frame** shares the header's 1280 px maximum width, including Att göra with its reading pane open. [Layout measures](layout-measures.md) records the column and its gutters.
- **The facts** of a thing's page are a 340 px end panel, a named complementary region; at 1024 px and below (`narrowPageQuery`) they stand above the read, as the `work-item-detail` template places them.
- **Att göra** measures its own column, as the `table-inbox` template does (D112 gap 4): below 900 px the list and the pane do not fit side by side, and a row leads to the item's page, with the queue as the way back; an item in the address there is replaced by its page. The pane opens at 55 % and is resizable, never narrower than 440 px nor the list than 300 px. On a phone the queue is a list of rows and not a table, the whole row the link to the item's page (D130 item 19, amended 2026-11-23).
- **A conversation** has the thing beside the transcript from 1200 px of window (500 px of transcript and the 440 px pane); narrower, the thing is a link on the header's line.
- **Kalender** has its entry's pane beside the grid from 1340 px; narrower, on a short screen and on a phone it is the drawer over the grid.
- **Tabs** (a thing's, a register's) are Astryx's `TabList` of links; on a phone the first stands and the others are under Astryx's `TabMenu` ("Fler"), so the strip fits 320 px.
- **A phone** is below 768 px (AppShell's `md`): Clew's pages are in the header's "Meny", a work item's page pins its next step at the foot with the rest under ⋯, a page's actions stand under its title, and Administration's sections are a list of their own at `/admin`.
- **A short screen** (480 px high or less, WCAG 1.4.10) pins nothing: every page scrolls as one.

## Placement and return rules

1. **A name is the way to its thing, and a place changes the page.** The header's places and pages, a name in any row, a title in a pane, a tile and a link that says where it goes all navigate. The name in a row is the link to the thing's page wherever the row is (D126 rule 1): a work item under "Behöver dig" on Hem, on a person's or a unit's page and in Sök; a person or a unit in every view; a conversation on Alla samtal. "Visa alla" under Behöver dig leads to Att göra, "Kalender" beside I dag to Kalender, "Alla nyheter" to `/tools/clew.news`, "Visa alla (N)" or "Enhetens sida" beside Läget to the unit's page. Whatever opens a list or a table leads to that view's page (rule 11): a tile of Läget, an answer's "Visa allt", a search hit, a row whose read is itself a list, a work item's read such as "Se i personalsystemet". A read of Clew's own may declare the page where its rows are worked on (`Capability.page`, `ViewEntry.page`, never a remote connector's): whatever opens it without input leads there instead, and the read's own page stays for an address that names it. `competence.attention` declares the matrix narrowed to what needs the manager (`/competence/matrix?only=attention`), so Kompetens's tile on Hem, the manager's card and an answer's "Visa allt" lead to the one face of those rows (the product owner, 2026-11-13). Any source's table of people may say that its rows are a read about a person asked for everyone (`view.byKey`, [D127](../decisions/D127-a-read-can-say-its-rows-are-a-read-about-people-asked-for-everyone.md)): where an administrator offers that read as a lens, whatever opens the table without input leads to Personal's tab for it, so Hem's figure "Sjukfrånvaro" is Personal's Frånvaro tab and a name there the person's page at that tab. What moves the person is the kind of view the source declares, never a choice in its manifest. A person's name always leads to the person, in every view: a row whose title names a person or a unit (`subject`) has its own read as a link of its own beside the name, never on it.
2. **A pane beside something exists in two places only** (D126 rule 2): beside Att göra's queue and beside a conversation. Each draws the same parts as the thing's page, its title the link to that page, its decisions under the title where they do not scroll away. Kalender's entry has a pane of its own beside the grid (D27), with its buttons under its name and, for a proposal, its name the link to its work item. Something opened from what belongs to no page (a reminder, a toast's "Visa", a hit in Sök) goes where a work item opened from the page the person is on goes (`cardOverPage`): in the queue, the item in the reading pane; anywhere else, the item's page with that page as the way back.
3. **Nothing opens over a page to show a thing** (D126 rule 3). The panel beside or over Hem, Personal, a tool, a person and a unit, the wide drawers of a person's talk and a unit's dialogue, and the read that unfolded inside a work item are gone. A work item's read is a link to the read's own page; the talk is a tab of the person's page and the dialogue a tab of the unit's. Every address that opened such a thing still works by leading to the thing's page, replaced in the history, with the page it lay over as the way back (`panelRedirect`, `overlayRedirect`, [Addresses](#addresses)).
4. **"Fråga Clew" starts a conversation about the thing** (D126 item 4). The assistant has two homes, the composer on Hem and the conversation's page; no other page has a box. "Fråga Clew", a ghost button among a thing's actions, leads to a new conversation (`/conversations/new`) with the thing beside it: a work item, a record, a person or a unit (on a source's tab, about that tab), a tool or a list by its chip, Kalender's range ("Fråga om veckan", om dagen, om månaden) or entry. Nothing is asked until the person writes, the box has the focus, and Esc leads back to the page it was pressed on. Without a model the button is not drawn (D8). The thing in the pane goes with every question asked there, and the question says that it stands open beside the transcript (`ConversationInput.beside`): the assistant is told so, and reads "det öppna ärendet", "kortet" or "rutan bredvid" as that thing. It is therefore never put in the box: "Lägg till i samtalet" is not drawn on the item in the pane. It is drawn where a conversation's own box is, on a work item an answer draws and on a row or a hit, which go into the box as a chip.
5. **Work a person goes through is a page or a tab, never a drawer over a page.** One person's talk ([D96](../decisions/D96-talks-is-the-first-owned-domain-the-round-and-action-points-behind-a-profile.md)) is the talk's tab on their page, a unit's follow-up dialogue ([D118](../decisions/D118-the-follow-up-dialogue-is-an-owned-domain.md)) the dialogue's tab on the unit's page, a person's own competences and plan tabs of their own page, the review of a work item's rows a page (below), a plan a page. The one overlay left is a unit over Organisation's tree ([D124](../decisions/D124-a-unit-is-walked-one-step-up-and-one-step-down-and-the-people-pages-follow-their-canvas.md)): walked a step up or down without closing, its steps replacing the address; a person's name in it leads to the person's page, whose way back keeps `unit`, so the way back and Back open the unit again.
6. **A write passes one path.** Every place an action's button is drawn opens the same action dialog (the work item in the pane, beside a conversation, on its page and on the phone's pinned bar; a person's or a unit's page; Personal's row menu; a plan's item; an assistant's draft). Nothing is sent before its last button. An action about a person or a unit opens the dialog before anything exists, over the page it was started on (D126 item 7): an execute's "Det här händer" is its effect read for the entity (`POST /api/entities/<kind>/<key>/effect`), and its last button makes the work item and the human decision at once (`POST /api/entities/<kind>/<key>/decide`, D45); a propose's last button drafts its work item with the answers; an action that asks nothing says what the capability does. The work item then opens on its own page, with the page the action was started on as its way back. ✕, Esc and Avbryt send nothing and leave no work item, no draft and no audit row; the values stay while the page stays (D112 gap 8). An assistant's draft opens the dialog at "Det här händer" with "Granska och …" and at "Uppgifter" with "Ändra". A work item's write with neither `asks` nor `effect` stays one press.
7. **Close lets go of the top layer only, and gives the focus back to what opened it.** A confirmation closes into its dialog, the dialog into the page, with the focus on the control that opened it. ✕ or Esc in Att göra's pane closes the item and gives the focus to its row; an item that left the pile with its own button (Avfärda, Inte nu) gives it to the next row. ✕ or Esc in a conversation's pane gives it to the box. Back, or a page's way back, from any page that was opened from a link gives the focus to that link once the page has drawn it (`useFocusOnTheWayBack`); a tab pressed keeps the focus on itself, now the current tab of the page it led to. A work item sent away with its own button on its page leads back to `from`. If completion removed the opener, the focus goes to the owning section. One exception ([D124](../decisions/D124-a-unit-is-walked-one-step-up-and-one-step-down-and-the-people-pages-follow-their-canvas.md)): after a step inside Organisation's unit drawer, closing gives the focus to the tree row of the unit shown, with the tree open down to it, not to the row or link that opened the drawer, which may be another unit's, far from where the person now stands. With no step taken the opener has it back, as everywhere else. Never send focus behind an open modal.
8. **The address is the state** (D126). The page, its tab (`section`, `show`, `tab`), the chosen item in the queue, what a conversation is about, a review's step, Kalender's range and entry, the unit over Organisation's tree, and Användare's filters and page are in the address, so Back, Forward and a reload reproduce them; the words typed in Användare's search are not, since the list sends them to the server in a body ([D100](../decisions/D100-the-people-list-is-read-a-page-at-a-time-and-narrowed-on-the-server.md)); `from` holds only a path inside Clew, never a `from` of its own. A cold link from before a rebuild leads to where the same thing is now and never guesses a prior page. The one exception is a person's link from before [D122](../decisions/D122-clew-keys-its-own-records-about-a-person-by-the-persons-id.md): it carries a user name, so it names nobody ([Addresses](#addresses)). The action dialog and a confirmation have no address: a reload never greets the person with a write they did not ask for. A module's own dialog may have one (Kompetens's `?add=`, `?register=`, `?approve=`, `?validate=`, Introduktion och avslut's `?start=`) when it writes nothing before its last button, so a reload or a link shows the form and never the write; once it has written or closed, its address is replaced or dropped, so Back never opens it again.
9. **Opening never decides.** Opening, closing or navigating approves nothing, and asks the model nothing. Current session, AI availability and source authorization apply at every read and decision. Pending confirmations are cancelled when their owning interaction closes.
10. **Out of Clew is a new tab, every time, marked as leaving** (D111 rule 8): a link with an address is Astryx's `Link` with `isExternalLink` (its icon and "(öppnas i ny flik)"), a work item's read with `target: 'link'` is a button with the `externalLink` icon named with the same words, and a news row with a `link` leaves the same way. The shared header's places are the one exception: they cross between the organisation's sites in the same tab (D88). Not yet marked: an answer's numbered citations and the links in its text, which Astryx's `Markdown` and `Citation` open in a new tab without the icon.
11. **A list, a table or a task a person works through is a page** ([D116](../decisions/D116-a-panel-is-a-peek-at-one-thing-a-list-is-a-page-and-every-view-has-an-address.md)), and since D126 so is a single thing. The product owner, 2026-11-12: "should an organisation really open in a sidebar? This can get extremely crammed, an organisation might have hundreds of employees. The sidebar needs to be for peeking or some kind of instant actions, otherwise a full route is needed", and "clicking on item should navigate in a way the user will find predictable". Every view has an address: `/tools/<capability>` for every read the person may open, placed in the "Verktyg" menu or not, with `from` as the way back named at the top of the page. A list or a table is a register there; a record, a text or a document a thing's page. Back from it returns to the page it was opened from with the focus on the link that opened it. Where a person already works in a list, the list keeps its companion: Att göra's reading pane.
12. **A system that does not answer is said where its content is missing** ([D116, amended 2026-11-12](../decisions/D116-a-panel-is-a-peek-at-one-thing-a-list-is-a-page-and-every-view-has-an-address.md)), never as a banner over the page. A work item from it says "kan vara gammal" at the end of its row's why line and, in its head, since when ("Kan vara gammal · Personalsystemet svarar inte sedan 14:32"); with nothing left to draw, Hem's "Behöver dig" and Att göra's empty pile name the system. A block or a page whose own read failed says so in itself with "Försök igen": "I dag" on Hem and Kalender's head ("Kalendern svarar inte", since when when known), as Läget's tiles, a person's and a unit's tabs, a tool's page and Personal do. The action dialog's last step, a `confirm` and a decision on its way say that what is sent is queued and read back ("Det du skickar köas och läses tillbaka när systemet svarar."). The first time a system is seen silent in a session it is said once, politely: "<system> svarar inte". A person with no calendar (the calendar not switched on for them, or no account bound) gets no warning: "I dag" and Kalender say so and who connects one, and the pages' row draws Kalender only while the calendar is the person's to see. Clew itself out of reach while a page is open is not a system's silence: nothing on the page is missing, so there is no place in it to say so, and a banner over the page is what this rule forbids. It is a toast without a time limit in the session's own toast region, Astryx's surface for a status that belongs to no part of the page: it covers nothing the person is working on, F6 reaches it, it closes by keyboard, and it goes by itself when Clew answers ([D46](../decisions/D46-browser-updates-use-committed-revisions.md); [From origin to close](#from-origin-to-close)). On the first load nothing is drawn yet, so what says it stands in the page's place.

**The review is a page** (2026-11-12, under rule 11; [D73](../decisions/D73-the-review-is-a-page-in-the-shell-with-an-address.md) as amended that day): `/items/<threadId>/review`, with `step` (1-based, rewritten in place) and `from` (the page the work item was shown on), reached by "Granska N" ("Fortsätt granska" once begun) and by each row under "Dokument" wherever the item is shown. Back from any step is the item where it was, with the focus on the control that led there; Skicka's answer and "Tillbaka till ärendet" go the same way. The decisions are the person's draft for the session in the tab ([review-session.ts](../../apps/web/src/review-session.ts)), so a reload or a way out and back keeps them. `?review=<threadId>&step=<n>` on any page and `/cards/<threadId>/review` lead to it.

A new interaction should be reviewable by naming its **origin, space, parent, close target, focus target and address**. If it fits none of the spaces above, amend the decision before introducing another. Do not resolve placement inside a provider component.
