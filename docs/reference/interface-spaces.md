# Interface spaces and navigation

Clew gives each space a stable job. **Choosing a place changes the page. Opening something from the page keeps that page, beside it or under it.** The space is decided by what the thing is, never by the page the control was on or by the source that supplied it. A URL identifies content; having its own URL does not make that content a page.

This is the current placement reference. [D83](../decisions/D83-interface-spaces-have-stable-navigation-roles.md) set the spaces, [D111](../decisions/D111-where-something-opens-is-decided-by-what-it-is.md) the rules that place things in them, and [D112](../decisions/D112-the-interface-is-rebuilt-on-astryx-templates-a-queue-one-detail-shape-one-panel.md) rebuilt every surface on one Astryx template each; where the three differ, D112 holds. Everything below was read from the code and the browser specs named under [Implementation and verification](#implementation-and-verification). Connector authors have a shorter version in [Where things open](../guides/where-things-open.md).

## The spaces

| Space | Its job | What it holds and how it behaves |
|---|---|---|
| Rail | Choose a place or an earlier conversation. | Astryx's `shell-side-nav`. Top to bottom: Ny fråga and Sök (Astryx's `CommandPalette` over the page); Hem, Att göra with the count of what needs the person, Kalender, Personal for a manager ([D94](../decisions/D94-a-manager-has-a-personal-section-listing-the-people-on-their-units.md)); Verktyg, the views an administrator placed (D40), each a page of its own (D42); Samtal, the conversations, newest first, a dot only where the data says something (the answer being written, a draft that waits); Administration and the person at the foot. Every row is a link, so Back and a reload land in the same place. It folds to icons; it is not resizable (D112 gap 1). With a shared header connector (D88) the header is Astryx's TopNav above the rail. On a phone the rail is AppShell's drawer. |
| Page | Where the person chose to be. | One main column per place: Hem (`/`, `ai-chat-landing`), Att göra (`/todo`, `table-inbox`), a work item's page (`/items/<threadId>`, `work-item-detail`), Kalender (`/calendar`), a tool (`/tools/<capability>`, `table-filter`), Alla nyheter (`/tools/clew.news`), Personal (`/staff`), a person (`/people/<key>`), a unit (`/units/<id>`), a conversation (`/conversations/<id>`, `ai-chat`) and Administration. A page's own content (Hem's blocks, Kalender's grid) is the page's, never something the panel holds. |
| Panel | Keep one thing beside the page, with the conversation about it. | The end slot. It holds one thing: a work item, a view or a record, Kalender's event or proposal, or the page itself after "Fråga Clew". Top: the header with the thing's name, "Fortsätt som samtal" once a conversation about it exists, and ✕ ("Stäng panelen"); under it the thing, then the conversation about it, then the box at the foot while a model is configured (without one the panel is the thing alone, D8). Opening another thing takes the place of what it held; there are no tabs. Opening sends nothing and moves the focus to the panel's heading. |
| Att göra's reading pane | Read and act on the chosen work item without leaving the queue. | The work item at its narrow width beside the list, as the panel draws a thing: the item on top, the conversation about it and the box. "Öppna som sida" leads to the item's page. Something opened from the item while it is in the pane lies in the drawer over the page, since the pane holds the end slot. |
| A work item's page | The item as a destination. | The same header and main column as the pane, plus the details rail (Detaljer, Berör, Öppna i källan). `from` is the way back. A person's and a unit's page use the same shape: every action in the header, every section one under the other, the directory's facts and "Tillhör" in the rail. |
| Conversation view | Continue a long exchange. | `/conversations/<id>`: the transcript is the column and the thing it is about is its artifact in the panel beside it. Asked on Hem, the transcript takes Hem's column; "Stäng samtalet" or Esc returns to the page it was asked from. |
| Focused overlays | Work through something while the page stays under it. | The review of a work item's rows one at a time ([D73](../decisions/D73-the-review-is-a-page-in-the-shell-with-an-address.md)) and one person's talk ([D96](../decisions/D96-talks-is-the-first-owned-domain-the-round-and-action-points-behind-a-profile.md)): Astryx's end `Drawer`, a tall `BottomSheet` on a phone, over the page and its panel. One at a time: an address that names both draws the review. |
| Action dialog | Fill in and confirm one write. | Astryx's `form-wizard-dialog` over the page, from every place the action's button is drawn, when the action's `path` asks something or has an `effect` (D112 item 10): "Uppgifter" when there is something to fill in, "Det här händer" always, last. Fullscreen on a phone with its buttons pinned at the foot. It has no address. |
| Confirmation and feedback | Decide about, or report on, the interaction that owns it. | A `confirm` is a small Dialog over the item (on the dialog's last step when the action opens the action dialog); receipts stay on the work item, row by row, with Ångra while the source allows it; the source's refusal stands under the buttons; a toast supplements durable state and never replaces it. |

## Widths

- **The panel beside the page** from one window width on every page that holds it: the rail, 640 px of column and the panel, 260 + 640 + 440 = 1340 px (`BESIDE_FROM`). Narrower, on a short screen (480 px high or less, WCAG 1.4.10) and on a phone it is the drawer over the page, with the same header. The page gives way, the panel does not: a page whose wide layout no longer fits beside the panel draws the narrow layout it already has (Personal its rows, a person's section its list, a tool its list), its own breakpoint shifted by the panel's width.
- **Administration** has no narrow layout, so its pages always get the panel as the drawer over the page.
- **Att göra** measures its own box, as the `table-inbox` template does (D112 gap 4): below 900 px of box the list and the pane do not fit side by side, and a chosen item takes the whole surface as its page draws it, with the way back to the list. The pane opens at 55 % and is resizable, never narrower than 440 px nor the list than 300 px.
- **The details rail** of a work item's, a person's and a unit's page (340 px) moves behind "Visa detaljer" in a fullscreen dialog at 1024 px and below, and while the panel holds something beside the page.
- **A phone** is below 768 px (AppShell's `md`): the rail is a drawer, a row opens the work item full screen with its next step pinned at the foot and the rest under "Fler val", the details are behind the information button, the overlays are sheets. The panel stays the end drawer, full height with Astryx's 56 px reveal of the page beside it; D112 item 12 draws it as a sheet, which is not built.
- **A short screen** pins nothing: in the panel the thing, the transcript and the box follow one another in one scroll; pages scroll as one.

## Placement and return rules

1. **Only choosing a place changes the page.** The rail, a name that leads to a person or a unit, and the few links that say they go to a place: a work item's row on Hem, "Läs" on Fokusnyhet and a reminder or a search hit picked on Hem all lead to the item in Att göra; "Öppna som sida" leads to the item's page; "Alla nyheter" to `/tools/clew.news`; Läget's unit name and "Öppna dialogstödet" to the unit's page. Nothing a source declares moves the person to another page.
2. **What is opened from a page opens in that page's panel.** A view, a record, a work item about a person or a unit, the work item an action about them makes: beside the page from 1340 px, the drawer over it below. Something opened from what belongs to no page (a reminder under the bell, a toast's "Visa", the rail's search) opens over the page the person is on, with that page's address kept: on Att göra and Hem it is the item in Att göra's reading pane, on the item's own page it is that page, anywhere else the work item in the panel (`?card=`).
3. **The panel holds one thing.** Opening another takes its place. A view opened from a row of a view in the panel takes the view's place with the way back ("Tillbaka", `via`). A work item's own read unfolds inside the item under its buttons (`open-card`, `open-action`, replaced in the history, not pushed); a read that answers with a table still takes the item's place in the panel, with ✕ back to the item.
4. **The panel asks about its thing.** The box at its foot asks about what the panel holds, and only that. "Fråga Clew" in a page's header makes the page the thing (`ask=1`): on Att göra (the queue, or the item in the pane, whose box takes the focus), a work item's page, a person's and a unit's page, a tool's page and Alla nyheter; on Kalender it is "Fråga om veckan" (or om dagen, om månaden). Hem has its own box and no "Fråga Clew"; Personal and Administration have neither. Another thing in the panel's place starts with an empty transcript; the earlier exchange stays under Samtal. "Fortsätt som samtal" moves the exchange to the conversation view with the thing beside it; Back returns to the panel as it was. "Lägg till i samtalet" is drawn only beside a conversation's own box (`/conversations/<id>`), where a work item, a row or a hit goes into the box as a chip.
5. **Focused work lies over the page it was started on.** The review and the talk each add their part to that page's address (`review` and `step`, `talk`) and close to the page as it was under them, its panel included. Something opened from an overlay is one level deeper: close returns to the overlay, then to the page.
6. **A write passes one path.** Every place an action's button is drawn opens the same action dialog (the work item in the pane, the panel, the item's page and the phone's pinned bar; a person's or a unit's page; Personal's row menu; an assistant's draft). Nothing is sent before its last button. An execute started on a person, a unit or Personal's row opens its work item beside the page (or over the list) with the dialog over it, on the item's own button; a propose that asks is filled in first and drafts its work item with the answers. ✕, Esc and Avbryt send nothing; the values stay while the work item is open (D112 gap 8). An assistant's draft opens the dialog at "Det här händer" with "Granska och …" and at "Uppgifter" with "Ändra". A write with neither `asks` nor `effect` stays one press.
7. **Close lets go of the top layer only, and gives the focus back to what opened it.** A confirmation closes into its dialog, the dialog into its work item, the review into the item with its decisions as the selection, the item in the panel into the page, the panel's view into the item it was opened from. ✕ or Esc in the panel returns the focus to the link or button that opened it, drawn again where the page took its own layout back; Esc in Att göra's pane closes the item and returns the focus to its row; if completion removed the opener, the focus goes to the owning section. Never send focus behind an open modal.
8. **The address is the state.** What the panel holds, the chosen item, a section, a lens, a review's step and a talk are in the address, so Back, Forward and a reload reproduce them; a move within a page (a person's section) keeps what is open beside it; `from` holds only a path inside Clew, never a `from` of its own. A cold link from before a rebuild leads to where the same thing is now and never guesses a prior page. The action dialog and a confirmation have no address: a reload never greets the person with a write they did not ask for.
9. **Opening never decides.** Opening, closing or navigating approves nothing, and asks the model nothing. Current session, AI availability and source authorization apply at every read and decision. Pending confirmations are cancelled when their owning interaction closes.
10. **Out of Clew is a new tab, every time, marked as leaving** (D111 rule 8): a link with an address is Astryx's `Link` with `isExternalLink` (its icon and "(öppnas i ny flik)"), a work item's read with `target: 'link'` is a button with the `externalLink` icon named with the same words, and a news row with a `link` leaves the same way. The shared header's places are the one exception: they cross between the organisation's sites in the same tab (D88). Not yet marked: an answer's numbered citations and the links in its text, which Astryx's `Markdown` and `Citation` open in a new tab without the icon.

A new interaction should be reviewable by naming its **origin, space, parent, close target, focus target and address**. If it fits none of the spaces above, amend the decision before introducing another. Do not resolve placement inside a provider component.

## A person and a unit

[D97](../decisions/D97-a-source-says-what-its-rows-are-about-and-clew-places-them.md) applies rules 1, 2 and 8 to entities ([Entities](../architecture/entities.md)). Personal is the destination; a person and a unit are pages, shown one way (D111 rule 3) in the work item's page shape (D112 item 5). No rail entry leads to a person or a unit, and nothing draws a person beside a list.

| User action | Origin | Space | Close target | Focus target | Address example |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Choose a lens | "Visa: Alla" on Personal | The list becomes the lens's view | "Alla" in the same menu | The Visa button, which names the lens | `/staff?show=talks.person` |
| Open the person | A name on Personal, or on any row, hit or work item that names them | The person's page | The way back to `from`: Personal for the reader's own people without one, Att göra for a work item's row | The page's heading | `/people/johan?from=%2Fstaff` |
| Open a person's work items | "Just nu" counting several ("2 att agera på") | The person's page at Behöver dig, the list as `from` | The way back | The section's heading | `/people/johan?section=needs-you&from=%2Fstaff` |
| Open one work item about them | "Just nu" counting one; a work item under Behöver dig on the person's or the unit's page | The panel beside that page | ✕ or Esc: the page with its lens, section and way back | Open: the panel's heading; close: the link that opened it | `/staff?card=<threadId>`, `/people/johan?section=needs-you&from=%2Fstaff&card=<threadId>` |
| Open a unit | "Läget på <enhet>" and "Öppna dialogstödet" on Hem; the unit under a person's name; a row that names it | The unit's page | The way back to `from` | The page's heading | `/units/unit-skola?from=%2F` |
| Start an action about them | The header's buttons, "Fler" by system, or "Åtgärder" on a phone; Personal's row menu | The work item it makes, in the panel beside the page (over the list on Personal), with the action dialog over it when its path asks or has an effect | ✕ or Esc: the page as it was | Close: the control that started it, on Personal the row's menu button | `/people/johan?from=%2Fstaff&card=<threadId>`, `/units/unit-skola?card=<threadId>` |
| Go to a section | `?section=` in a link; Personal's count; a talk's way back | The page, scrolled to the section | — | The section's heading, unless the panel or an overlay holds the focus | `/people/johan?section=hr.person` |
| See the directory's facts | The details rail; "Visa detaljer" at 1024 px and below or while the panel is open | A fullscreen dialog | ✕ or Esc | "Visa detaljer" | — |

A person or a unit the reader may not ask about is the page's own "Personen finns inte" or "Enheten finns inte", the same answer as an unknown key. A row on the entity's own page that names the same entity is plain text, not a link back to the page the reader is on. The talks source's way in is a query alone (`?talk={id}`), which Clew adds to the page it is drawn on; a way in that is a path (the unit's "Visa i Personal") is a page of its own.
