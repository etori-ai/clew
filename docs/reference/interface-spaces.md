# Interface spaces and navigation

Clew gives each space a stable job. **Choosing a place changes the page. Opening something from the page keeps that page, beside it or under it.** The space is decided by what the thing is, never by the page the control was on or by the source that supplied it. A URL identifies content; having its own URL does not make that content a page.

This is the current placement reference. [D83](../decisions/D83-interface-spaces-have-stable-navigation-roles.md) set the spaces, [D111](../decisions/D111-where-something-opens-is-decided-by-what-it-is.md) the rules that place things in them, and [D112](../decisions/D112-the-interface-is-rebuilt-on-astryx-templates-a-queue-one-detail-shape-one-panel.md) rebuilt every surface on one Astryx template each; where the three differ, D112 holds. [D116](../decisions/D116-a-panel-is-a-peek-at-one-thing-a-list-is-a-page-and-every-view-has-an-address.md) (2026-11-12) narrowed the panel to a peek at one thing: a list or a table is a page wherever it is opened from, and every view has an address. Everything below was read from the code and the browser specs named under [Implementation and verification](#implementation-and-verification). Connector authors have a shorter version in [Where things open](../guides/where-things-open.md).

## Widths

- **The panel beside the page** from one window width on every page that holds it: the rail, 640 px of column and the panel, 256 + 640 + 440 = 1336 px (`BESIDE_FROM`). Narrower, on a short screen (480 px high or less, WCAG 1.4.10) and on a phone it is the drawer over the page, with the same header. The page gives way, the panel does not: a page whose wide layout no longer fits beside the panel draws the narrow layout it already has (Personal its rows, a person's section its list, a tool its list), its own breakpoint shifted by the panel's width.
- **Administration** has no narrow layout, so its pages always get the panel as the drawer over the page.
- **Att göra** measures its own box, as the `table-inbox` template does (D112 gap 4): below 900 px of box the list and the pane do not fit side by side, and a chosen item takes the whole surface as its page draws it, with the way back to the list. The pane opens at 55 % and is resizable, never narrower than 440 px nor the list than 300 px.
- **The details rail** of a work item's, a person's and a unit's page (340 px) moves behind "Visa detaljer" in a fullscreen dialog at 1024 px and below, and while the panel holds something beside the page.
- **A phone** is below 768 px (AppShell's `md`): the rail is a drawer, a row opens the work item full screen with its next step pinned at the foot and the rest under "Fler val", the details are behind the information button, the overlays are sheets. The panel stays the end drawer, full height with Astryx's 56 px reveal of the page beside it; D112 item 12 draws it as a sheet, which is not built.
- **A short screen** pins nothing: in the panel the thing, the transcript and the box follow one another in one scroll; pages scroll as one.

## Placement and return rules

1. **Choosing a place, or a list, changes the page.** The rail, a name that leads to a person or a unit, and the links that say they go to a place: "Alla N i Att göra" on Hem leads to Att göra; "Öppna som sida" leads to the item's or the record's page; "Alla nyheter" to `/tools/clew.news`; Läget's unit name and "Öppna dialogstödet" to the unit's page. Whatever opens a list or a table leads to that view's page (rule 11): a tile of Läget, a suggestion under the box, an answer's "Visa allt", a search hit, a row whose read is itself a list, a work item's read such as "Se i personalsystemet". What moves the person is the kind of view the source declares, never a choice in its manifest.
2. **One thing opened from a page opens in that page's panel.** A detail, a record, a text, a figure, a work item (on Hem: a row under "Behöver dig", "Läs" on Fokusnyhet, the day's link to an item, an item suggested under the box; [D112, amended 2026-11-12](../decisions/D112-the-interface-is-rebuilt-on-astryx-templates-a-queue-one-detail-shape-one-panel.md)), a work item about a person or a unit, the work item an action about them makes: beside the page from 1336 px, the drawer over it below. Something opened from what belongs to no page (a reminder under the bell, a toast's "Visa", the rail's search) opens over the page the person is on, with that page's address kept: on Att göra it is the item in the reading pane, on the item's own page it is that page, anywhere else, Hem included, the work item in the panel (`?card=`).
3. **The panel holds one thing, and never drills.** Opening another takes its place; there is no "Tillbaka" inside the panel and no `via` (D116). A row inside the thing in the panel leads a list to its page and one thing into the thing's place. A work item's own read unfolds inside the item under its buttons (`open-card`, `open-action`, replaced in the history, not pushed); a read that answers with a table or a list is a link to its page instead, and one that turns out to answer with rows when it arrives leads there.
4. **The panel asks about its thing.** The box at its foot asks about what the panel holds, and only that. "Fråga Clew" in a page's header makes the page the thing (`ask=1`): on Att göra (the queue, or the item in the pane, whose box takes the focus), a work item's page, a person's and a unit's page, a tool's page and Alla nyheter; on Kalender it is "Fråga om veckan" (or om dagen, om månaden). Hem has its own box and no "Fråga Clew"; Personal and Administration have neither. Another thing in the panel's place starts with an empty transcript; the earlier exchange stays under Samtal. "Fortsätt som samtal" moves the exchange to the conversation view with the thing beside it; Back returns to the panel as it was. A list or a table asked about with "Fråga Clew" on its page is not drawn beside the conversation (D116): the conversation keeps it as its chip, and the list stays its own page. "Lägg till i samtalet" is drawn only beside a conversation's own box (`/conversations/<id>`), where a work item, a row or a hit goes into the box as a chip.
5. **Focused work lies over the page it was started on.** One person's talk and a unit's dialogue ([D118](../decisions/D118-the-follow-up-dialogue-is-an-owned-domain.md)) each add their part to that page's address (`talk`, `dialog`) and close to the page as it was under them, its panel included. Something opened from an overlay is one level deeper: close returns to the overlay, then to the page. The review of a work item's rows, which lay over the page until 2026-11-12, is a page under rule 11 (below).
6. **A write passes one path.** Every place an action's button is drawn opens the same action dialog (the work item in the pane, the panel, the item's page and the phone's pinned bar; a person's or a unit's page; Personal's row menu; an assistant's draft). Nothing is sent before its last button. An execute started on a person, a unit or Personal's row opens its work item beside the page (or over the list) with the dialog over it, on the item's own button; a propose that asks is filled in first and drafts its work item with the answers. ✕, Esc and Avbryt send nothing; the values stay while the work item is open (D112 gap 8). An assistant's draft opens the dialog at "Det här händer" with "Granska och …" and at "Uppgifter" with "Ändra". A write with neither `asks` nor `effect` stays one press.
7. **Close lets go of the top layer only, and gives the focus back to what opened it.** A confirmation closes into its dialog, the dialog into its work item, the item in the panel into the page, the panel's view into the item it was opened from. ✕ or Esc in the panel returns the focus to the link or button that opened it, drawn again where the page took its own layout back; Esc in Att göra's pane closes the item and returns the focus to its row; if completion removed the opener, the focus goes to the owning section. Back from the review's page is the item where it was, with its decisions as the selection and the focus on the control that led there. Never send focus behind an open modal.
8. **The address is the state.** What the panel holds, the chosen item, a section, a lens, a review's step and a talk are in the address, so Back, Forward and a reload reproduce them; a move within a page (a person's section) keeps what is open beside it; `from` holds only a path inside Clew, never a `from` of its own. A cold link from before a rebuild leads to where the same thing is now and never guesses a prior page. The action dialog and a confirmation have no address: a reload never greets the person with a write they did not ask for.
9. **Opening never decides.** Opening, closing or navigating approves nothing, and asks the model nothing. Current session, AI availability and source authorization apply at every read and decision. Pending confirmations are cancelled when their owning interaction closes.
10. **Out of Clew is a new tab, every time, marked as leaving** (D111 rule 8): a link with an address is Astryx's `Link` with `isExternalLink` (its icon and "(öppnas i ny flik)"), a work item's read with `target: 'link'` is a button with the `externalLink` icon named with the same words, and a news row with a `link` leaves the same way. The shared header's places are the one exception: they cross between the organisation's sites in the same tab (D88). Not yet marked: an answer's numbered citations and the links in its text, which Astryx's `Markdown` and `Citation` open in a new tab without the icon.
11. **A panel, a drawer or a sheet is for peeking or an instant action; a list, a table or a task a person works through is a page** ([D116](../decisions/D116-a-panel-is-a-peek-at-one-thing-a-list-is-a-page-and-every-view-has-an-address.md)). The product owner, 2026-11-12: "should an organisation really open in a sidebar? This can get extremely crammed, an organisation might have hundreds of employees. The sidebar needs to be for peeking or some kind of instant actions, otherwise a full route is needed", and "clicking on item should navigate in a way the user will find predictable". A panel, a drawer or a sheet holds one thing to look at, or an action done at once. A list that can grow, or something a person works in, gets a full route with an address of its own, and every view has one: `/tools/<capability>` for every read the person may open, placed in the rail or not, with `from` as the way back named at the top of the page. Back from it returns to the page it was opened from with the focus on the link that opened it. A link to the full page inside the panel is allowed where the thing is already there ("Öppna som sida"), never as the only way to do anything. Where a person already works in a list, the list keeps its companion: Att göra's reading pane and a tool's inspector. Administration › Organisation was the first place changed for it: a unit's people were a drawer over the tree (`?unit=<unitId>`, a sheet on a phone) and are now the unit's own page, where they are a table that is searched and paged, while the tree's row stays the peek (who leads the unit and how many are on it). The review was weighed the same day and is a page (below); one person's talk, which a person works through in a drawer over the page (rule 5), has not been weighed against this rule yet ([open questions](../maintainers/open-questions.md)).
12. **A system that does not answer is said where its content is missing** ([D116, amended 2026-11-12](../decisions/D116-a-panel-is-a-peek-at-one-thing-a-list-is-a-page-and-every-view-has-an-address.md)), never as a banner over the page. A work item from it says "kan vara gammal" at the end of its row's why line and, in its head, since when ("Kan vara gammal · Personalsystemet svarar inte sedan 14:32"); with nothing left to draw, Hem's "Behöver dig" and Att göra's empty pile name the system. A block or a page whose own read failed says so in itself with "Försök igen": "I dag" on Hem and Kalender's head ("Kalendern svarar inte", since when when known), as Läget's tiles, a person's and a unit's sections, a tool's page and Personal already did. The action dialog's last step, a `confirm` and a decision on its way say that what is sent is queued and read back ("Det du skickar köas och läses tillbaka när systemet svarar."). The first time a system is seen silent in a session it is said once, politely: "<system> svarar inte". A person with no calendar (the calendar not switched on for them, or no account bound) gets no warning: "I dag" and Kalender say so and who connects one, and the rail draws Kalender only while the calendar is the person's to see.

**The review is a page** (2026-11-12, under rule 11; [D73](../decisions/D73-the-review-is-a-page-in-the-shell-with-an-address.md) as amended that day, the rule recorded the same day in [D116](../decisions/D116-a-panel-is-a-peek-at-one-thing-a-list-is-a-page-and-every-view-has-an-address.md)): `/items/<threadId>/review`, with `step` (1-based, rewritten in place) and `from` (the page the work item was shown on), reached by "Granska N" ("Fortsätt granska" once begun) and by each row under "Dokument" wherever the item is shown. Back from any step is the item where it was, with the focus on the control that led there; Skicka's answer and "Tillbaka till ärendet" go the same way. The decisions are the person's draft for the session in the tab ([review-session.ts](../../apps/web/src/review-session.ts)), so a reload or a way out and back keeps them. `?review=<threadId>&step=<n>` on any page and `/cards/<threadId>/review` lead to it.

A new interaction should be reviewable by naming its **origin, space, parent, close target, focus target and address**. If it fits none of the spaces above, amend the decision before introducing another. Do not resolve placement inside a provider component.

## A person and a unit

[D97](../decisions/D97-a-source-says-what-its-rows-are-about-and-clew-places-them.md) applies rules 1, 2 and 8 to entities ([Entities](../architecture/entities.md)). Personal is the destination; a person and a unit are pages, shown one way (D111 rule 3) in the work item's page shape (D112 item 5). No rail entry leads to a person or a unit, and nothing draws a person beside a list.

| User action | Origin | Space | Close target | Focus target | Address example |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Choose a lens | "Visa: Alla" on Personal | The list becomes the lens's view | "Alla" in the same menu | The Visa button, which names the lens | `/staff?show=talks.person` |
| Open the person | A name on Personal, or on any row, hit or work item that names them | The person's page | The way back to `from`: Personal for the reader's own people without one, Att göra for a work item's row | The page's heading | `/people/johan?from=%2Fstaff` |
| Open a person's work items | "Just nu" counting several ("2 att agera på") | The person's page at Behöver dig, the list as `from` | The way back | The section's heading | `/people/johan?section=needs-you&from=%2Fstaff` |
| Open one work item about them | "Just nu" counting one; a work item under Behöver dig on the person's or the unit's page | The panel beside that page | ✕ or Esc: the page with its lens, section and way back | Open: the panel's heading; close: the link that opened it | `/staff?card=<threadId>`, `/people/johan?section=needs-you&from=%2Fstaff&card=<threadId>` |
| Open a unit | "Läget på <enhet>" and "Öppna dialogstödet" on Hem; the unit under a person's name; a row that names it | The unit's page | The way back to `from` | The page's heading | `/units/unit-skola?from=%2F` |
| Start an action about them | The header's buttons, "Fler" by system, or "Åtgärder" on a phone; Personal's row menu | The work item it makes, in the panel beside the page (over the list on Personal), with the action dialog over it when its path asks or has an effect | ✕ or Esc: the page as it was | Close: the control that started it, on Personal the row's menu button | `/people/johan?from=%2Fstaff&card=<threadId>`, `/units/unit-skola?card=<threadId>` |
| Go to a section | `?section=` in a link; Personal's count; a talk's way back | The page, scrolled to the section | — | The section's heading, unless the panel or an overlay holds the focus | `/people/johan?section=hr.person` |
| See the directory's facts | The details rail; "Visa detaljer" at 1024 px and below or while the panel is open | A fullscreen dialog | ✕ or Esc | "Visa detaljer" | — |

A person or a unit the reader may not ask about is the page's own "Personen finns inte" or "Enheten finns inte", the same answer as an unknown key. A row on the entity's own page that names the same entity is plain text, not a link back to the page the reader is on. The talks source's way in is a query alone (`?talk={id}`), which Clew adds to the page it is drawn on; a way in that is a path (the unit's "Visa i Personal") is a page of its own.
