# UX review of the whole interface

2026-11-05, session 43. The product owner asked for a full review of the interface at the level of a professional UX
designer: every part captured and judged for how professional it looks, how cluttered it is and how it can be nicer,
"extremely clear, usable and accessible", without leaving Astryx. Parts felt cluttered or a bit boring. The review came
first, with two patches and a Swedish report with before and after images. The product owner then approved every finding
and the direction, and asked for all of it to be fixed. The direction is decision D48, and everything below is on `main`.

## Method

- A production build with `/api` mocked by the WCAG harness (`tests/a11y`): synthetic data, a fixed clock, one person.
  `tests/a11y/screens.review.spec.ts` (run with `CLEW_SCREENS=1`) writes 100 captures at 1600, 1280, 1024, 768 and 375 px
  and at 1280×1900, some of them dark, and per capture what can be counted: tab stops, words, type sizes. Six audits
  measure status-dot contrast, target sizes and text contrast.
- Graded against `bunx astryx docs is-my-code-good`, the component guidance read with `bunx astryx component`, and
  WCAG 2.2 AA.
- Causes were read in the code before anything was called a defect.

## Verdict

The foundation is good: semantic structure, contrast of text, strict use of Astryx, dialogs and the landing page all
hold. The problem is amount, not quality. The same fact is said up to five times on one page, the design's reasoning
is printed as captions, and every thread carries every control. The page reads as a report about itself rather than a
list of what to do. It got that way one reasonable decision at a time, which is why it can be undone without changing
the product's model.

The "boring" part is mostly a hierarchy problem. When five buttons are black and 70 of 142 text nodes are 12 px,
nothing stands out, so nothing feels designed. Keeping black for what is due today did more than any added colour would
have. The Sundsvall profile shows what one accent adds on top.

## Findings

### Clutter on the overview and the area page (fixed)

- **One fact, five places.** The same counts appeared in the rail, the h1 sentence, the group headings, "Vid sidan av"
  and the brief line. The right column repeated the rail.
- **The design's reasoning was printed as content**: "alltid samma fyra", "Bara det som har en dag får en dag", "Ingen
  deadline, nej tack är ett svar", "Ingen knapp, försvinner när du läst", "Så här bevakar jag". They explain the design
  to a reviewer, not the page to a person using it.
- **Three tiers of header** before the first figure, and "8 trådar lästa", which nobody needs.
- **Six controls on every thread**: primary, secondary, `@`, Inte nu, Påminn mig, ⋯. A row took three lines.
- **The word for why** was a Token up to six words long. Astryx: "keep labels 1–3 words". The reason could be reached
  three ways: the token, ⋯ and "Varför nu?".
- **Colour without meaning.** Area tokens in up to eight colours, against "stick to two or three meaningful colors", so
  the one red that means something competed with them.
- **Type hierarchy**: a 14 px card title under a 17 px group heading, with the "Jag föreslår" card at 17 px.
- **"Inget behöver dig just nu" over three rows** while the rail said 2. The heading meant "nothing urgent".
- **On a phone** the first card started at y≈490 of 667, behind the docked box.

### The rail (fixed)

- 27 tab stops, 79 words, seven groups, in mixed voices (Från mig, Jag föreslår, Inte nu under Pågår).
- Thread titles as content, truncated, all linking to the same pile.
- Status as a yellow dot alone, with the meaning in a tooltip. That fails 1.4.1 and 1.4.11, and a tooltip does not
  exist on touch.
- Black "Ny fråga" was a no-op on the landing and always the strongest thing on the page.
- Icons only below the fold of the rail.

### The box (fixed)

- About 110 px tall on the overview and the piles, with a scope line, a shortcut and a model name. "Kapabel" is a name in
  the test data: an administrator names a model, so that is not the product's word.

### The second round, in the order they were ranked

Each of these was found in the review, listed as not yet prototyped, and fixed after the product owner's go-ahead.

1. **Touch targets.** 75 of 77 targets on the overview were under 44 px. The theme's `adaptations` now raise every control
   to 44 px under a coarse pointer, with list rows, segments and disclosure rows, and a mouse keeps the compact sizes.
   `tests/a11y/touch.spec.ts` measures every page with a touch device.
2. **Conversation.** Three left edges (272, 365, 418 px) became two: the title stands on the box's text edge (382 px) and
   the answer 36 px inside it, which is `ChatLayout`'s own spacious column. The title is smaller and capitalised. "Gör till
   ett kort", primary and jargon, became "Lägg under Behöver dig", secondary. The source is named once. "2 rader under sök i
   telefonboken. Se dem i vyn." became "Sök i telefonboken: 2 rader."
3. **Telefonbok.** A table from 1280 px, one row per person, so a 1280 px laptop fits about three times as many. Under
   that, the numbers and the address share a line. The stops are unchanged: each number is still a link.
4. **Administration.** The tiles are gone and the filter carries the counts. "9 · 3 åtgärder · 5 till assistenten" became
   "9 funktioner / 3 utför · 5 för assistenten". Ta bort stands apart. Seven of 13 metadata fields fold under Tekniska
   uppgifter, Källa is gone. The Verktyg switch stands apart from the form, forms are at most 560 px and Standard is a
   neutral badge. Manifest, Förmågor, Genomgång, Clew and router gave way to plain words.
5. **Side panel.** Half of Astryx's scrim, the date as a date, and a line saying where the read came from. The `@` on a row
   found its place in the table's Samtal column.
6. **Calendar.** A panel in another week says so first, with the way there. A week's meeting shows its name, not a time and
   half a name, with a smaller radius. A phone's head is two rows, the title with Förslag and Ny bokning and the steps with Vy, and holds under a finger too. Boka says what is missing.
7. **Handled.** Time and type are two choices, and the time leads the line.
8. **Waiting.** The progress bar is gone, the Stepper holds the sum, and "0 ritade" counts what was drawn. The 9.6 px step
   number is inside Astryx and stays.
9. **Appearance.** Samma som datorn is the first choice and the default.
10. **Words.** Kort instead of tråd, also in the server's error messages. Nytt samtal instead of Ny fråga, which also ends the
    clash with "Vad vill du göra?". Sammanfattning instead of samling. The landing's brief has no `@`.
11. **Inte nu and Påminn mig.** Not merged. They are two behaviours, D28's pile and D17's timer, so one picker is a product
    decision. Each sub-menu now says what it does: `lägg undan` and `kortet ligger kvar`.

The reminders Drawer was also found too heavy, but `feature/notiser-och-kontonamn` already replaces it with a bell
Popover (uncommitted in the product owner's tree), so it is not reported as new. Its work overlaps this one in
`apps/web/src/components/shell.tsx`, `apps/web/src/components/nav.tsx`, `HANDOVER.md`, `docs/decisions/` and
`tests/a11y/shell.spec.ts`.

### Found when the result was checked

The last look at the finished screens found what the work itself had caused. The 15 px base made text wider, and neither
the suite nor the measures above can see that:

- "Löneadministratör" broke in the middle at 1280 px. The floors in `apps/web/src/components/view.tsx` were set for one
  long word less. They are now 130, 145, 118, 248 and 66 px, 970 in all, and the table starts at 1280 px.
- The primary button "Anslut system" was cut to "Anslut syst…" at 1280 px, because a long lead took the room. In
  `PageLead` the words now take what is left.
- "Adress och nyckel" was cut in the dialog's Stepper, "Inbjudningar" in a card's Stepper at 768 and 1024 px, and
  `raindance.overview` broke in two.
- Two row titles were cut at 768 and 1024 px. A title there is a button, whose label cannot wrap, so under 1200 px the row's
  step goes under its line.
- "Utvecklingssamtal · Anna" broke in the middle in a week. The person now has a line of their own.
- "Attestera 3 fakturor, 48 200 kr" was cut in "Menar du" on a phone, and now wraps.

The same look showed that this review had claimed too much: the phone calendar's head was three rows, not two, and 44 px controls would not have fitted the two it was built for. It is now two, with the proposals button moved up beside Ny bokning. It also found the previous-week button named "Förra veckaen", which only a screen reader hears, now "Förra veckan".

So `tests/a11y/screens.review.spec.ts` now records, for every capture and the whole height of the page, the text cut off
with an ellipsis and every word on two lines. After the fixes no word is broken in any of the 100 captures. What is still
cut: a meeting's name in a week's narrow block (the block is what it is, the dialog has the name) and a long conversation
title in the rail (a row of its own width). Hidden text, the rail's `Alltid` heading and a block's time and kind, also
shows up in the list and is cut for nobody. `tests/a11y/card-actions.spec.ts` holds these cases, and `tests/a11y/tools.spec.ts`
the table.

## The direction, as approved (D48)

- **Type:** `typography.scale` in `packages/ui/src/theme.ts`, base 15 at ratio 1.2. One line, the whole ramp moves, and
  the documented seam. It also made five layouts fail at 320 px and in the 1.4.12 spacing test, all fixed. Each was a
  layout already at its limit.
- **Overview:** the h1 first, one short line under it, the policy sentence behind `InfoTip`; group captions gone;
  groups at heading level 2, card titles at level 3; the right column is the day and "Klart i dag" alone; on a phone the
  groups come before the figures.
- **Cards and rows:** a row is a title, one line (the same line as the landing's brief) and the one next step, with the
  rest behind ⋯. A card shows primary, secondary, Inte nu and ⋯. `Lägg till i samtalet` and `Påminn mig` move behind ⋯,
  as words, with `DropdownMenuSubMenu`. Area tokens become plain words.
- **Black means today:** `primary` only where `streamGroup` is `nu`. Everything else is a secondary button, including
  the sidebar's "Nytt samtal" and the card's "Skicka".
- **Rail:** three pile rows in one group (Pågår utan dig, Inte nu, Redan hanterat), five latest conversations in one
  group that says when, icons on every top-level row, a number in place of the status dot.
- **Box:** on the overview and the piles no scope line, shortcut or model; the placeholder says "Nytt samtal:". The model is
  still on the landing and in a conversation.
- **Week agenda:** folds rather than cuts Friday off; booked meetings are plain lines, what is new stays a token.
- **Words:** "Inget brådskar just nu".
- **Assistant's knowledge:** `apps/server/src/assistant/context.ts` lists the buttons the assistant may name, so it
  moved with them.

## Verification

- `bun run check` passes on the tree merged with `main`: typecheck, dependency rules, purity, 340 unit tests, the Node tests and the generated documents.
- `tests/a11y` on the final code: Neutral 266 pass and 141 skipped, Sundsvall 266 pass and 142 skipped. The skipped ones
  are the 107 capture scenarios and the other profile's branding tests. Both ran at the same time on the same machine.
- One intermittent failure: the model dialog test in `tests/a11y/models.spec.ts` failed twice in about ten full runs,
  once on each profile, in axe's contrast check on the button "Lägg till 1 modell". The likely cause is that the button
  fades from disabled to enabled in 125 ms and axe measured it halfway under load; that was not reproduced on demand.
  The test now waits for the fade to end.
- New specs: `tests/a11y/card-actions.spec.ts` (the keyboard path to everything behind ⋯, one filled button, the InfoTip,
  phone order, side panel, Redan hanterat, booking, the calendar on a phone), `tests/a11y/touch.spec.ts` (44 px under a
  finger) and `tests/a11y/appearance.spec.ts` (Samma som datorn).
- One harness change: `hiddenFocusStops` in `tests/a11y/support.ts` now waits 150 ms when a tooltip is on screen.
  Measured: a tooltip stays about 100 ms after the focus leaves, and Playwright tabs faster than that. It measures what
  stays, as the wait for a settling focus ring does. It should be read as a change to the test, not a pass earned by the
  UI.
- `heading()` in the same file finds a heading by its level, because Playwright's `getByRole` reads the tag and ignores
  `aria-level`. The conversation's title and the phone calendar's month are `h3` elements at level 1, which Astryx's
  `accessibilityLevel` writes as `aria-level`.
- `Toolbar` keeps one row, so the action rows on the model and provider pages wrap on a phone, as
  `apps/web/src/pages/admin/connector.tsx` already does.

## Decisions amended

## Not inspected

- **Real data.** Everything is synthetic, and long names, many cards or an empty organisation may behave differently.
  Signing in needs Keycloak, which was not touched.
- **The design canvases.** They need a claude.ai sign-in in the browser pane. CLAUDE.md asks for a comparison before a
  screen is called done, so that comparison is still owed.
- **A screen reader.** The suite proves structure, not experience. CLAUDE.md requires a manual pass for new flows, which
  the ⋯ sub-menu is.
- **Real devices.** Phone captures are an emulated 375 px viewport. Touch was not tried by hand.
- **Provider dialogs in `/admin`** beyond their screenshots.
- **Whether the changes help.** Fewer words and stops are measures of amount. Whether people find their way is a usability
  test with real staff.
